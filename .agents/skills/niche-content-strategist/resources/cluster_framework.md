# Topical Authority Cluster Framework

## The 3-Tier Content Model for Fast Google Indexing & Ranking

### Tier 1: Core Pillar (The Hub)
- **Format**: Single Deep-Dive Article (`article-publisher`)
- **Word Count**: 2,500 - 4,000 words
- **Target Query**: Broad high-volume head keyword (e.g., "Ultralight Backpacking", "Home Espresso", "Puppy Training")
- **Structure**: Comprehensive overview covering terminology, gear checklist, step-by-step framework, FAQs, and links to all Tier 2 & Tier 3 articles.

### Tier 2: Commercial Listicles & Roundups (High Revenue)
- **Format**: Ranked Listicles (`listicle-publisher`)
- **Word Count**: 1,800 - 2,500 words
- **Target Query**: Commercial investigation with "best", "top", "reviews" (e.g., "10 Best Ultralight Tents Under 3 lbs", "7 Best Espresso Grinders for Beginners")
- **Monetization**: High affiliate CTR, comparison tables, product ranking badges.
- **Link Target**: Links up to the Tier 1 Pillar guide; receives links from Tier 3 guides.

### Tier 3: Problem-Solving How-To Single Articles (Long-Tail Traffic)
- **Format**: Single Deep-Dive Article (`article-publisher`)
- **Word Count**: 1,200 - 1,800 words
- **Target Query**: Low-competition, high-intent specific questions (e.g., "Why is My Espresso Shot Sour? (5 Quick Fixes)", "How to Patch an Inflatable Sleeping Pad on the Trail")
- **Engagement**: High dwell time, featured snippet capture, high ad impressions.
- **Link Target**: Links directly to relevant products reviewed in Tier 2 listicles and the Tier 1 Pillar.
