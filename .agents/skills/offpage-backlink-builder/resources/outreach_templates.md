# High-Converting Outreach Email Templates for Backlinks

## 1. Broken Link Replacement Email (40%+ Response Rate)

**Subject:** Quick heads up regarding a broken link on {{TARGET_SITE_NAME}}

```text
Hi {{FIRST_NAME}},

I was browsing your article on {{TOPIC_TITLE}} this morning and found it super helpful, especially the section on {{SPECIFIC_SECTION}}.

While reading through, I noticed that one of the resources you referenced:
👉 "{{BROKEN_LINK_ANCHOR_TEXT}}" (pointing to {{BROKEN_URL}})
...is currently throwing a 404 dead link error.

We recently published a fresh, in-depth guide on the exact same topic:
🔗 {{YOUR_ARTICLE_TITLE}}: {{YOUR_ARTICLE_URL}}

It includes {{KEY_FEATURE_OR_STAT}} and is completely up to date for {{YEAR}}. If you'd like to replace the dead link to keep your readers from hitting a broken page, feel free to link to it!

Either way, keep up the fantastic work on {{TARGET_SITE_NAME}}.

Best regards,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
Founder, {{YOUR_BLOG_NAME}}
```
