# Pinterest & Social Promoter Skill

This skill creates high-CTR Pinterest pin graphics and viral social snippets to drive immediate referral traffic to your newly published articles and listicles.

## When to Use This Skill
- Right after publishing a new listicle or single guide
- When repromoting existing catalog articles on Pinterest, Flipboard, or Instagram
- When creating batch social promo graphics without needing Photoshop or Canva

## Promotion Workflow

When invoked with an article title or slug:
1. **Analyze Article & Identify 3 Angles**:
   - **Angle 1: Numbered Listicle Hook** (e.g., *"7 Best [Items] That Are Actually Worth Your Money"*).
   - **Angle 2: Curiosity & Avoid Mistake Hook** (e.g., *"The #1 Mistake Most Beginners Make With [Topic] (And How to Fix It)"*).
   - **Angle 3: Quick Transformation Hook** (e.g., *"How to [Achieve Desired Result] Without [Common Pain Point]"*).

2. **Generate Pinterest Copy**:
   - **Pin Title**: Under 100 characters, front-loaded with primary keywords.
   - **Pin Description**: 200–350 characters explaining why the reader needs to click, ending with a clear CTA (e.g., *"Tap to read our full field-tested review and rankings!"*).
   - **Hashtag Matrix**: 5–8 targeted niche hashtags.
   - **Target Board Ideas**: 3 relevant Pinterest board categories.

3. **Generate 2:3 Vertical Pin Graphic**:
   Creates an ultra-clean HTML/CSS pin preview file (`assets/pins/<slug>-pin-1.html`):
   - 1000px &times; 1500px standard vertical aspect ratio.
   - High-contrast text overlay using the blog's theme colors.
   - Category pill badge and website domain footer watermark.
   - Clean gradient or image background.
