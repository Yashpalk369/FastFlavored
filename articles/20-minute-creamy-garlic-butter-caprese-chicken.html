20-Minute Creamy Garlic Butter Caprese Chicken Skillet

Italian summer comfort in a 20-minute skillet: tender golden chicken breasts smothered in a rich garlic cream sauce, topped with bubbling fresh mozzarella, blistered cherry tomatoes, and basil.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy garlic butter Caprese chicken, season 4 thin chicken cutlets (1.25 lbs) with Italian herbs, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a cast iron skillet over medium-high heat; sear chicken for 3.5 minutes per side until golden brown, then transfer to a plate. In the same pan, sauté 4 minced garlic cloves and 1.5 cups whole sweet cherry tomatoes for 2 minutes until tomatoes begin to blister and burst. Lower heat to medium, pour in 1/2 cup chicken broth, 3/4 cup heavy cream, and 1/3 cup grated parmesan cheese; simmer for 2 minutes until glossy. Return chicken to the skillet, top each cutlet with a thick slice of fresh mozzarella cheese, cover skillet with a lid for 2 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbling. Drizzle with thick balsamic glaze and scatter fresh basil leaves.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Sweet Blistered Cherry Tomato Burst: Sautéing whole cherry tomatoes until their skins burst releases sweet acidic juices directly into the garlic cream sauce.
  • Melted Fresh Mozzarella Blanket: Covering the pan with a lid for 120 seconds captures steam, melting fresh mozzarella into a gooey, stretchy blanket over the chicken without baking.
  • Balsamic Glaze Contrast: A final zigzag drizzle of concentrated balsamic glaze provides a sweet-tart punch that cuts through the rich cream and cheese.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season & Sear Chicken: Pat chicken cutlets dry with paper towels. Season both sides with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add cutlets and sear for 3.5 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through (165°F). Transfer chicken to a plate.
  2. Blister Tomatoes & Garlic: Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining 1 tbsp butter, minced garlic, and cherry tomatoes to the skillet drippings. Sauté for 2 minutes, pressing lightly on a few tomatoes with a wooden spoon so they blister and burst their juices.
  3. Build Garlic Cream Sauce: Pour in chicken broth, heavy cream, and grated parmesan cheese. Whisk continuously for 2 minutes as the sauce bubbles into a luxurious, velvety cream.
  4. Melt Mozzarella on Chicken: Nestle the seared chicken cutlets back into the bubbling sauce. Place a thick slice of fresh mozzarella on top of each cutlet. Cover the skillet tightly with a lid or foil for 2 minutes until the mozzarella is completely melted, gooey, and bubbling.
  5. Finish with Balsamic & Basil: Remove lid and take skillet off heat. Drizzle the thick balsamic glaze in a zigzag over the melted cheese and tomatoes. Scatter fresh torn basil leaves on top and serve hot straight from the skillet!

Elena’s Balsamic Glaze Shortcut

Buy a bottle of prepared store-bought balsamic glaze (crema di balsamico) from the vinegar aisle! It has the syrupy, concentrated consistency of 30-minute reduced balsamic vinegar, ready to drizzle instantly without boiling vinegar on a busy weeknight.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this low carb / keto?
Yes! Naturally low in carbs at only 8g per serving, just ensure your store-bought balsamic glaze has no added high-fructose corn syrup, or use a splash of regular balsamic vinegar.
What can I serve with Caprese chicken?
Serve over buttered angel hair pasta, alongside roasted garlic broccoli, or with crusty warm Italian bread to mop up the garlic cream sauce.
Can I use chicken thighs instead?
Yes! Boneless skinless chicken thighs sear beautifully and remain extraordinarily juicy in this recipe.
