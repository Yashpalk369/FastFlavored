15-Minute Crispy Bang Bang Shrimp Tacos

Crunchy golden shrimp tossed in creamy sweet chili sriracha sauce, loaded into charred corn tortillas with refreshing lime slaw and jalapeños.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy Bang Bang shrimp tacos, whisk 1/3 cup mayonnaise with 3 tbsp sweet chili sauce, 1 tbsp sriracha, honey, and lime juice. Toss dry peeled shrimp in cornstarch, salt, and pepper, then shallow-fry in 3 tbsp hot oil for 2 minutes per side until golden and crispy. Toss hot shrimp in the creamy sauce, pile into charred warm corn tortillas with shredded lime cabbage slaw, and garnish with sliced jalapeños and cilantro.

Ingredients

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix Sauce & Slaw: Whisk mayonnaise, sweet chili sauce, sriracha, honey, and 1 tbsp lime juice until smooth. Toss cabbage slaw with remaining lime juice and salt.
  2. Crisp Shrimp: Pat shrimp completely dry. Toss in cornstarch with salt and pepper. Heat 3 tbsp oil in skillet over medium-high heat. Fry shrimp 2 minutes per side until golden and crunchy.
  3. Toss in Sauce: Gently fold hot crispy shrimp with 2/3 of the bang bang sauce.
  4. Char Tortillas: Warm tortillas over a hot dry pan or open flame for 10–15 seconds per side until lightly charred.
  5. Assemble Tacos: Fill tortillas with crunchy lime slaw and sauced crispy shrimp. Drizzle remaining sauce on top, garnish with jalapeño and cilantro, and serve!

Elena’s Instant Cornstarch Crunch

Shake Off Excess Cornstarch: When coating shrimp, you want a whisper-thin veil of cornstarch, not heavy clumps! Place your dried shrimp and starch into a fine-mesh strainer or colander and shake gently over the sink. This removes all excess powdery starch, allowing the shrimp to fry into a wafer-crisp glass shell in hot oil!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make the crispy shrimp in an air fryer?
Yes! Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (204°C). Place the cornstarch-coated shrimp in a single layer in the basket, spray generously with avocado oil spray, and air fry for 6 to 8 minutes, shaking halfway through. Toss with sauce immediately!
Can I make bang bang sauce lighter?
Substitute whole-milk Greek yogurt for half or all of the mayonnaise! It gives the sauce a refreshing tang and cuts down calories while preserving the creamy, sweet-and-spicy flavor.
Corn vs flour tortillas for shrimp tacos?
Charred white corn tortillas are the traditional choice for coastal fish and shrimp tacos because their earthy corn sweetness complements spicy seafood. Small flour tortillas also work wonderfully if you prefer a softer, chewier wrap.
