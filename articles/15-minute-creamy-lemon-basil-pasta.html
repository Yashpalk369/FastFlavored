Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make authentic 15-minute creamy lemon basil pasta (pasta al limone), whisk 1 cup of whole-milk ricotta with grated lemon zest, 3 tablespoons lemon juice, 3/4 cup parmesan cheese, and olive oil in a large bowl. Emulsify by whisking in 1/2 cup of hot starchy pasta cooking water until silky and smooth, then immediately toss with hot al dente fettuccine and fresh torn basil.

Why Ricotta Beats Heavy Cream in Lemon Pasta

  • Zero Curdling Risk: Lemon juice can cause heavy cream to curdle when heated. Ricotta naturally loves citrus and creates a light, creamy texture without separating.
  • No Heavy Stovetop Simmering: The sauce is whisked in a bowl and warmed entirely by the hot pasta and cooking water—no dirty pan required!
  • Aromatic Lemon Oils: Microplaning zest directly into the cheese captures volatile citrus oils that perfume the entire kitchen.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil Pasta: Cook fettuccine in boiling salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta cooking water, then drain.
  2. Whisk Sauce: In a large serving bowl, whisk ricotta, lemon zest, lemon juice, grated parmesan, olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper until creamy.
  3. Emulsify: Whisk 1/2 cup hot pasta water into the ricotta mixture until glossy and velvety.
  4. Toss: Add drained pasta directly to the bowl. Toss vigorously for 1 minute to coat every strand, adding 1 to 2 extra tbsp of water if desired.
  5. Garnish: Fold in fresh basil leaves and top with shaved parmesan and cracked black pepper. Serve warm!

Elena’s Zest-First Rule

Zest Before You Juice: Always zest your lemons with a microplane before cutting into them! Once a lemon is sliced or juiced, grating the delicate yellow peel becomes ten times harder. The bright yellow zest holds the fragrant lemon oils that give this dish its vibrant aroma.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you keep ricotta smooth and not grainy in pasta sauce?
Use whole-milk ricotta at room temperature, and whisk it thoroughly with hot starchy pasta water before tossing with noodles. Whisking emulsifies the milk curds with the starch, creating an ultra-silky cream texture.
Can I add protein to this lemon pasta?
Yes! Grilled chicken breast strips, sautéed garlic shrimp, or flaked wild salmon pair gorgeously with the bright citrus and herb flavors of pasta al limone.
