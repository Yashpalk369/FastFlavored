Creamy Garlic Butter Tuscan Chicken

Tender golden cutlets nestled in a silky parmesan-garlic sauce speckled with tangy sun-dried tomatoes and tender wilted spinach. A restaurant-worthy meal on the table in 25 minutes flat.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make the ultimate Tuscan chicken in 25 minutes, slice large breasts into thin cutlets so they sear in just 4 minutes per side without drying out. Build the velvety sauce in the same pan using browned pan fond, minced garlic, chicken broth, heavy cream, and freshly grated parmesan on low heat to guarantee the sauce never breaks or separates.

Recipe Highlights at a Glance

  • Single Pan Magic: Everything from searing the chicken to simmering the luscious cream sauce happens in one 12-inch skillet.
  • Restaurant Quality, Pantry Ease: Sun-dried tomatoes and freshly microplaned parmesan create deep umami without complicated reductions.
  • Diet Friendly: Naturally low-carb and keto-compliant (only 8g net carbs per serving) with effortless dairy-free adaptations.

Why This Recipe Works So Well for Weeknights

Most creamy chicken recipes suffer from one of two fatal flaws: either the chicken breast is thick and rubbery by the time the center cooks through, or the heavy cream sauce separates into an oily, curdled slick.

By butterflying or cutletting the chicken breasts horizontally, we double the surface area for a deeply caramelized, savory crust while slashing cooking time by more than 50%. The rendered fond (the golden browned bits stuck to the bottom of your skillet) is then immediately deglazed with chicken broth, infusing the entire cream sauce with deep roasted poultry flavor.

Chef's Secret for Golden Crust: Always pat your chicken cutlets thoroughly dry with paper towels before seasoning. Moisture is the enemy of a golden sear. When wet chicken meets hot fat, it steams instead of searing!

Key Ingredients & Simple Substitutions

Here are the core components you’ll need, along with smart kitchen swaps if your pantry is missing something:

  • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breasts: Look for organic breasts, or substitute boneless skinless chicken thighs (increase sear time by 2 minutes).
  • Oil-Packed Sun-Dried Tomatoes: Drain well, but save a tablespoon of the seasoned oil from the jar to sear your chicken for extra flavor!
  • Heavy Whipping Cream: Contains 36% milkfat, which prevents curdling when combined with the acidic tomatoes. For dairy-free, substitute full-fat culinary coconut cream.
  • Parmigiano-Reggiano: Grate it fresh from the block. Pre-shredded bagged cheese contains potato starch and cellulose anti-caking agents that make sauces grainy.
  • Fresh Baby Spinach: Wilt in the final 2 minutes so it keeps its bright green color and tender bite.

Chef Elena’s 3 Golden Rules for Flawless Pan Sauces

  1. Control the Heat During Dairy Addition: Always reduce your skillet from high heat down to medium-low before pouring in the cream. High heat shocks cold dairy and causes protein coagulation.
  2. Let the Chicken Rest: Once seared to 165°F (74°C), let the cutlets rest on a cutting board while you finish the sauce. This locks in the natural juices rather than leaking them out into the pan.
  3. Add Cheese Off the Heat: Remove the skillet from the burner completely right before whisking in the parmesan. Residual heat will melt it silkily without burning the dairy solids.

Creamy Garlic Butter Tuscan Chicken

Tested and approved by Elena Bennett • Serves 4

Prep Time 10 Mins
Cook Time 15 Mins
Total Time 25 Mins
Yield 4 Servings
Calories 485 kcal

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Prep Chicken: Pat chicken cutlets dry. Season both sides evenly with garlic powder, Italian herbs, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Sear to Golden: In a large 12-inch skillet, melt 1 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp olive oil over medium-high heat. Sear chicken cutlets 4-5 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through (165°F). Transfer to a plate.
  3. Aromatics: Drop heat to medium. Add remaining 1 tbsp butter to the pan. Sauté minced garlic and sun-dried tomatoes for 60 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Build Sauce: Deglaze with chicken broth, scraping up browned bits. Pour in heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer for 3 minutes.
  5. Melt & Wilt: Turn heat to low. Whisk in grated parmesan until completely smooth. Stir in baby spinach until just wilted (about 60 seconds).
  6. Assemble: Return chicken cutlets and resting juices to the skillet. Spoon sauce over the chicken, garnish with fresh basil, and serve hot!

What to Serve with Tuscan Chicken

Because the garlic parmesan cream sauce is so rich and flavorful, you’ll definitely want something on the side to soak up every drop:

  • Classic Pasta: Tossed with fettuccine, tagliatelle, or penne rigate.
  • Garlic Bread or Warm Baguette: Perfect for dipping straight into the pan.
  • Fluffy White Rice or Orzo: Absorbs the cream sauce like a dream.
  • Low-Carb Options: Roasted asparagus, steamed broccoli florets, or cauliflower mash.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you keep Tuscan chicken sauce from curdling?
Use full-fat heavy cream rather than low-fat milk, keep the skillet at a gentle simmer rather than a rolling boil, and remove the pan from direct high heat before whisking in finely grated real Parmigiano-Reggiano.
Can I use milk or half-and-half instead of heavy cream?
Half-and-half can be used if you whisk in 1 teaspoon of cornstarch before heating to stabilize it. Do not use regular skim or 2% milk, as the acid in the sun-dried tomatoes will cause it to separate.
How long do leftovers keep in the fridge?
Store leftovers in an airtight glass container for up to 4 days. Reheat gently in a small skillet over medium-low heat with a splash of broth or cream to restore its velvety texture.
