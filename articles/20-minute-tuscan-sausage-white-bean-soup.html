20-Minute Creamy Italian Sausage and Tuscan White Bean Soup

Zuppa Toscana comfort in 20 minutes: savory browned Italian sausage, velvety white beans, and dark curly kale simmering in a creamy parmesan garlic broth with crusty sourdough bread.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy Italian sausage and Tuscan white bean soup, heat a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown 1 lb sweet or mild Italian sausage, breaking it into bite-sized crumbles for 5 minutes until crispy; remove half the rendered fat. Add 1 diced yellow onion and 4 minced garlic cloves; cook for 2 minutes. Pour in 4 cups low-sodium chicken bone broth and two 15-oz cans of drained, rinsed cannellini beans. Lightly mash 1/3 of the beans with a potato masher to naturally thicken the broth. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and stir in 3 packed cups of chopped Tuscan or curly kale and 1/2 cup heavy cream. Simmer for 4 minutes until kale is tender. Finish with 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese and crushed red pepper flakes. Serve with toasted rustic sourdough.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Cannellini Starch Thickening: Crushing a cup of tender cannellini beans directly in the pot releases natural bean starches that transform thin broth into a hearty, velvety chowder consistency.
  • Italian Sausage Fond Base: Browning sausage meat builds deep savory caramelized fond on the bottom of the Dutch oven that deglazes into the broth for rich, slow-simmered taste in minutes.
  • Kale Tenderizing Simmer: Chopped lacinato or curly kale wilts down into tender, silky ribbons in just 4 minutes of gentle simmering while holding vibrant dark green color.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Brown the Sausage: Heat a large Dutch oven or heavy soup pot over medium-high heat. Add the Italian sausage and cook for 5 minutes, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon into bite-sized morsels until browned and crispy at the edges. (Drain excess fat if needed, leaving 1 tbsp in the pot).
  2. Sauté Onion & Garlic: Add diced yellow onion, minced garlic, oregano, thyme, and red pepper flakes to the sausage. Sauté for 2 minutes until onion is softened and translucent.
  3. Add Broth & Mash Beans: Pour in chicken bone broth and the drained cannellini beans. Bring to a rolling boil. Use a potato masher or the back of a large spoon to gently crush about one-third of the beans against the side of the pot to thicken the soup.
  4. Simmer Kale & Cream: Reduce heat to medium-low. Stir in chopped kale and heavy cream. Simmer gently for 4 minutes until the kale is tender and wilted into the rich broth.
  5. Stir in Parmesan & Serve: Turn off heat. Stir in freshly grated parmesan cheese until melted throughout. Taste and season with additional salt and black pepper as desired. Ladle into warm bowls and serve with thick, crusty slices of toasted sourdough bread for dipping!

Elena’s Parmesan Rind Simmer Hack

Whenever you finish a wedge of real Parmigiano-Reggiano, save the hard cheese rind in a bag in your freezer! Tossing a frozen parmesan rind into this soup as it boils infuses deep, savory umami and glutamates that make a 20-minute weeknight soup taste like it simmered all afternoon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use spinach instead of kale?
Yes! Fresh baby spinach wilts in just 60 seconds; simply stir it in right at the end when adding the parmesan cheese.
Can I freeze this soup?
Soups with cream can sometimes separate when frozen. To freeze, freeze the soup before adding heavy cream; thaw, bring to a simmer, and stir in cream and parmesan fresh before serving.
Is this soup similar to Olive Garden's Zuppa Toscana?
Yes! It has the same beloved flavor profile of Italian sausage, creamy broth, and kale, but swaps slow-cooking sliced potatoes for high-protein, creamy cannellini beans for a faster weeknight dinner.
