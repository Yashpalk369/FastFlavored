Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make restaurant-quality creamy tomato tortellini in 15 minutes, simmer refrigerated fresh cheese tortellini directly in crushed San Marzano tomatoes and broth for 4 minutes. Finish with heavy cream, real Parmigiano-Reggiano, and fresh baby spinach off the burner so the dairy emulsifies smoothly into a glossy sauce.

Why You Will Love This Recipe

  • No Separate Water Boiling: Drop fresh pasta directly into the simmering sauce to absorb flavor deep inside.
  • Vegetarian Comfort: Hearty, comforting, and deeply satisfying without requiring any meat.
  • Under 15 Minutes: Faster than ordering and waiting for food delivery.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sauté Aromatics: In a deep skillet or Dutch oven, warm olive oil and butter over medium heat. Sauté minced garlic for 60 seconds until fragrant.
  2. Build Simmering Broth: Add crushed San Marzano tomatoes and broth. Season with salt and pepper. Bring to a gentle rolling boil.
  3. Cook Tortellini: Add fresh cheese tortellini directly to the pan. Cook uncovered for 4-5 minutes until plump and tender.
  4. Cream & Cheese: Lower heat to low. Stir in heavy cream and grated parmesan cheese until velvety.
  5. Wilt Spinach: Fold in baby spinach and stir for 60 seconds until just wilted.
  6. Garnish: Top with torn fresh basil and extra shaved parmesan. Enjoy hot!

Chef Elena’s One-Pot Secrets

Always Choose Refrigerated Tortellini: Refrigerated tortellini cooks in under 5 minutes without turning mushy. Dried boxed tortellini takes nearly 14 minutes and requires significantly more liquid. Look for fresh pasta in the dairy/cheese section of your grocery store!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need to boil tortellini before adding to sauce?
No! That is the beauty of this one-pot recipe. Fresh refrigerated tortellini cooks directly in the tomato-broth simmer in just 4-5 minutes, releasing natural starches that thicken the sauce.
Can I add meat to this recipe?
Yes! Brown 1/2 pound of sweet or spicy Italian ground sausage before adding the garlic, or stir in shredded rotisserie chicken along with the cream.
