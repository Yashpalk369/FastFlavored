20-Minute Sheet-Pan Honey Garlic Glazed Pork Tenderloin Medallions

Juicy, caramelized pork tenderloin medallions roasted on one sheet pan with fresh green beans and baby carrots, basted in an irresistible sticky honey garlic soy glaze in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan honey garlic pork tenderloin, slice 1.25 lbs pork tenderloin into 1-inch thick medallions. Whisk 1/3 cup honey, 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 4 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp olive oil, and 1 tsp grated fresh ginger. Place medallions, 8 oz trimmed green beans, and 1 cup baby carrots onto a rimmed baking sheet; brush pork generously with half the sauce. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 14 minutes (145°F internal pork temp). Brush with remaining simmered glaze and broil 2 minutes for sticky caramelization.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • 1-Inch Medallion Speed Hack: Slicing lean pork tenderloin into rounds slashes cooking time from 35 minutes down to 14 minutes, preventing dry overcooking.
  • Sticky Broil Caramelization: The combination of natural honey sugars and soy sauce forms a deeply lacquered, glossy glaze under the oven broiler.
  • Single-Pan Vegetable Sync: Tender green beans and halved baby carrots roast in the exact same 14-minute window to a blistered, tender-crisp bite.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat & Prep Sheet Pan: Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large heavy-duty rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil or parchment paper and lightly mist with cooking spray.
  2. Whisk Honey Garlic Glaze: In a small saucepan or microwave-safe bowl, combine honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, grated ginger, and red pepper flakes. Heat for 60 seconds until bubbling and syrupy. Reserve 3 tablespoons of glaze in a separate small dish for post-bake baste.
  3. Season Medallions & Vegetables: Arrange sliced pork medallions in the center of the baking sheet; season with salt and pepper. Arrange green beans and baby carrots around the pork. Drizzle vegetables with 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt. Brush the pork generously on all sides with the remaining glaze.
  4. Roast to Perfection: Roast at 425°F for 12–14 minutes until the pork medallions reach an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) and vegetables are tender-crisp.
  5. Broil, Glaze & Garnish: Turn oven to High Broil. Brush pork with the reserved sticky glaze and broil for 2 minutes until glossy and bubbling with caramelized edges. Let rest for 2 minutes, sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and chives, and serve immediately!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Always pull pork tenderloin at 145°F internal temperature (which shows a slight rosy blush in the center) and let it rest for 2 full minutes before carving or serving. This ensures maximum juiciness without moisture pooling on your plate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know when pork medallions are done?
Use an instant-read meat thermometer inserted into the thickest medallion. At 145°F (63°C) with a 2-minute rest, pork is exceptionally tender, juicy, and safe to eat.
Can I use frozen green beans?
Fresh green beans are heavily recommended as frozen green beans release excess water that steams rather than roasts on the sheet pan.
Can I meal prep this dish?
Yes! Roasted pork medallions and vegetables store wonderfully in meal prep containers for up to 4 days in the refrigerator. Reheat gently in the microwave or toaster oven.
