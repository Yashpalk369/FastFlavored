20-Minute Creamy Tuscan Garlic Butter Shrimp and Gnocchi

Plump seared jumbo shrimp and pillowy potato gnocchi simmered in a luscious sun-dried tomato, garlic, spinach, and parmesan cream sauce in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy Tuscan shrimp and gnocchi, sear 1 lb peeled jumbo shrimp in 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter for 2 minutes per side until pink; remove to a plate. In the same skillet, melt 1 tbsp butter and sauté 4 minced garlic cloves and 1/3 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes for 1 minute. Pour in 1 cup chicken broth and 3/4 cup heavy cream; bring to a simmer. Add 16 oz shelf-stable potato gnocchi straight from the package; cook uncovered for 4 minutes until pillowy. Stir in 2 cups baby spinach and 1/2 cup grated parmesan until melted. Return shrimp to warm through.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Uncooked Gnocchi Simmer: Cooking shelf-stable potato gnocchi directly in seasoned cream and broth plumps them into tender pillows while starch thickens the sauce.
  • Quick Shrimp Sear Preservation: Pulling seared shrimp from the skillet while the gnocchi cooks prevents the delicate seafood from overcooking and turning rubbery.
  • Tuscan Sun-Dried Tomato Punch: Oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes bring concentrated sweetness and tart acidity that balances heavy cream and rich parmesan.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sear Jumbo Shrimp: Pat shrimp completely dry; season with a pinch of salt and pepper. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add shrimp in a single layer and sear for 2 minutes per side until pink and caramelized. Transfer shrimp to a clean plate.
  2. Sauté Aromatics & Sun-Dried Tomatoes: Melt remaining 1 tbsp butter in the same skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic, red pepper flakes, and sliced sun-dried tomatoes; sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Simmer Gnocchi in Cream Broth: Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream, scraping up the browned shrimp bits from the bottom. Bring to a steady simmer. Add the uncooked potato gnocchi straight from the package into the bubbling liquid. Cook uncovered for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi are tender and pillowy and sauce begins to thicken.
  4. Wilt Spinach & Melt Parmesan: Stir in fresh baby spinach and grated parmesan cheese. Gently fold for 90 seconds until spinach wilts into emerald ribbons and cheese melts into a velvety cream sauce.
  5. Return Shrimp & Serve: Nestle seared shrimp back into the creamy skillet to warm through for 60 seconds. Scatter fresh torn basil on top and serve piping hot straight from the skillet with lemon wedges!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Do not boil the gnocchi in a separate pot of water! Cooking dry gnocchi directly in the simmering heavy cream and chicken broth allows the potato starches to leach into the sauce, naturally thickening it into a luxurious velvety glaze that coats every spoonful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen gnocchi?
Yes! Frozen gnocchi take about 6 minutes to simmer tender in the cream broth. Add an extra 2 tablespoons of chicken broth.
Can I substitute chicken for shrimp?
Yes! Thinly sliced chicken breast cutlets or tenderloins sear in 3–4 minutes and pair exquisitely with Tuscan cream sauce.
How do I reheat leftovers?
Reheat gently in a small skillet over low heat with a splash of milk or cream for 3–4 minutes until warm. Avoid high microwave heat as it toughens shrimp.
