15-Minute Creamy Coconut Lime Shrimp Curry

Succulent jumbo shrimp poached in a luscious, fragrant coconut curry broth with ginger, tender spinach, and a vibrant squeeze of fresh lime.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute creamy coconut lime shrimp curry, sauté 2 tablespoons curry paste with minced garlic and grated ginger in coconut oil for 1 minute. Pour in 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk, 1 tablespoon fish sauce, and lime zest; simmer for 3 minutes. Add 1 lb peeled jumbo shrimp and baby spinach, simmering for 3 to 4 minutes until the shrimp are opaque and pink. Remove from heat and stir in fresh lime juice before serving.

Why This Curry Works Wonders

  • Gentle Poaching: Poaching shrimp directly in the hot coconut broth prevents them from turning rubbery or dry.
  • Naturally Dairy-Free: Canned full-fat coconut milk creates a silky, decadent texture with zero cream or butter.
  • One-Pan Pantry Magic: Built entirely in one skillet using staple curry paste, canned coconut milk, and freezer shrimp.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Bloom Paste: Heat coconut oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Sauté curry paste, garlic, and ginger for 1–2 minutes until deeply fragrant.
  2. Simmer Broth: Whisk in coconut milk, fish sauce, brown sugar, and lime zest. Bring to a gentle simmer for 3 minutes to blend the aromatics.
  3. Poach Shrimp & Spinach: Add jumbo shrimp and spinach. Simmer gently for 3–4 minutes until shrimp are pink and opaque (145°F).
  4. Finish with Lime: Remove skillet from the heat. Stir in 2 tablespoons of fresh lime juice to preserve bright citrus flavor.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Top with fresh cilantro and sliced red chilies. Ladle into bowls over hot jasmine rice or rice noodles!

Elena’s Off-Heat Citrus Rule

Always Add Lime Off the Heat: Never boil fresh lime juice in coconut curry! High heat turns citrus juice dull and bitter. By taking the skillet off the flame right before squeezing in the fresh lime juice, you preserve the bright, floral acidity that cuts through the rich coconut milk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen shrimp for this curry?
Yes! Thaw the frozen shrimp by placing them in a colander under cool running water for 5 minutes. Pat them completely dry with paper towels before adding them to the simmering broth so excess water doesn't dilute the curry.
How spicy is this coconut lime shrimp curry?
Yellow curry paste is very mild and warming, making it family-friendly. If using Thai red curry paste, it will have a pleasant medium kick. You can adjust the spice level easily by altering the curry paste quantity or omitting sliced fresh chilies.
What can I substitute for fish sauce?
If you don't have fish sauce, substitute with an equal amount of low-sodium soy sauce or tamari mixed with a pinch of salt to provide that essential savory umami backbone.
