20-Minute Sheet-Pan Sizzling Flank Steak Fajitas

Juicy, caramelized sliced flank steak strips roasted with sweet bell peppers and onions, served with warm tortillas and zesty lime in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan steak fajitas, slice 1.25 lbs flank steak against the grain into 1/2-inch strips. In a large bowl, toss steak, 2 sliced bell peppers, and 1 sliced sweet onion with 2 tbsp avocado oil, 1.5 tbsp fajita seasoning (chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt), and juice of 1 lime. Spread evenly in a single layer on a preheated heavy baking sheet. Roast at 450°F (230°C) for 10 minutes, then broil on High for 2–3 minutes until steak edges are charred and peppers are tender-crisp. Serve in warm flour tortillas with guacamole and pico de gallo.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Preheated Baking Sheet Sizzle: Placing steak and peppers on a baking sheet preheated in the oven delivers an instant restaurant sizzle without stove splatters.
  • Against-the-Grain Slicing: Slicing lean flank steak across the muscle fibers breaks tough strands, ensuring melt-in-your-mouth tender fajita strips in minutes.
  • High-Heat 450°F Roast + Broil: Cooking at 450°F cooks flank steak to a juicy medium-rare while charring pepper edges into sweet, smoky perfection.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat Sheet Pan in 450°F Oven: Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C). Place an empty rimmed metal baking sheet on the middle rack while the oven heats up (preheating the metal pan creates an instant sear when food touches it).
  2. Slice & Season Fajita Mix: Slice flank steak against the grain into 1/2-inch wide strips. In a large mixing bowl, combine sliced steak, bell peppers, and onions. Drizzle with avocado oil and lime juice, then toss with chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
  3. Spread on Blazing Hot Pan: Carefully pull the hot baking sheet from the oven using oven mitts. Immediately spread the steak, pepper, and onion mixture across the pan in a single even layer (you will hear a satisfying sizzle).
  4. Roast & Broil to Charred Perfection: Roast at 450°F for 9 to 10 minutes. Turn oven to High Broil for the final 2 minutes until steak edges are crisp and browned and peppers have blistered char marks.
  5. Warm Tortillas & Serve Cantina Style: Wrap tortillas in damp paper towels and microwave 30 seconds (or char over open gas burner). Squeeze fresh lime juice over the sizzling sheet pan, sprinkle with fresh cilantro, and serve right at the table with warm tortillas, guacamole, and salsa!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Put your baking sheet in the oven while it preheats! When you dump your seasoned flank steak and cold peppers onto a 450°F preheated metal sheet pan, the contact sear triggers instantaneous caramelization, preventing watery vegetables and locking juices into the steak.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I slice flank steak correctly?
Look closely at the steak to find the direction of the muscle fibers (the grain). Always cut perpendicular across those lines—never parallel! Cutting against the grain shortens meat fibers so every bite is tender.
Can I make chicken fajitas using this method?
Yes! Substitute sliced boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs. Roast at 450°F for 12–14 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
How do I store and reheat leftovers?
Store cooled fajita filling in an airtight container for up to 4 days. Reheat in a smoking hot dry skillet for 2 minutes to restore the sizzling charred edges.
