Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make crispy sheet-pan gnocchi with sausage in 25 minutes, toss uncooked shelf-stable potato gnocchi, sliced smoked kielbasa, and bell peppers in 3 tablespoons of olive oil. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 20 minutes in a single layer until the gnocchi skin blisters crispy and golden brown without boiling.

Why This Method Changes Everything

  • No Boiling Required: No heavy pots of boiling water, no colanders, zero steaming mess.
  • Sensational Texture: Roasting caramelizes the exterior starches, creating a crispy crust with a soft potato interior.
  • Only 5 Primary Items: Gnocchi, sausage, bell peppers, onion, and olive oil.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat: Heat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large rimmed sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Toss Everything Raw: Scatter uncooked gnocchi, sausage coins, bell peppers, and red onions across the pan.
  3. Season: Drizzle with olive oil, then sprinkle Italian herbs, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Toss until evenly glistening.
  4. Spread Flat: Arrange everything into a single layer with space between items for crisp roasting.
  5. Roast: Bake for 20-25 minutes, flipping once halfway, until gnocchi are golden-brown and crisp.
  6. Finish: Sprinkle immediately with grated parmesan cheese and fresh rosemary. Serve hot!

Chef Elena’s Roasting Secrets

Do Not Overcrowd the Pan: If ingredients are piled on top of each other, trapped steam will make the gnocchi soft instead of crispy. Use an extra-large 18x13 half-sheet pan or divide across two pans if needed!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need to boil gnocchi before roasting on a sheet pan?
No! Roasting shelf-stable or fresh gnocchi raw at 425°F yields a delightful contrast: crispy golden-brown edges with a pillowy, tender center that boiled gnocchi simply cannot match.
Can I use cauliflower or gluten-free gnocchi?
Yes! Frozen Trader Joe's cauliflower gnocchi can be roasted directly from frozen. Just add 5 additional minutes to the roasting time until golden.
