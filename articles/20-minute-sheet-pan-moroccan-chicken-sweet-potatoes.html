20-Minute Sheet-Pan Moroccan Spiced Chicken and Roasted Sweet Potatoes

Warm fragrant spices meet sheet-pan convenience: juicy boneless chicken thighs roasted with diced sweet potatoes, cumin, cinnamon, and smoked paprika, served with cool garlic herb yogurt.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan Moroccan spiced chicken and sweet potatoes, dice 1 large sweet potato into small 1/2-inch cubes and microwave in a covered bowl with 1 tbsp water for 3 minutes to par-cook. Toss 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs and the par-cooked sweet potatoes with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1.5 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp black pepper. Spread across a large baking sheet with 1 sliced red onion. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 14 minutes, switching to high broil for the final 2 minutes until chicken edges are charred and sweet potatoes are caramelized. Drizzle with garlic herb yogurt and scatter with fresh pomegranate seeds.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Pre-Microwaved Sweet Potato Trick: Dense sweet potatoes usually take 35 minutes to roast; par-cooking small 1/2-inch cubes in the microwave for 3 minutes slashes roasting time to just 14 minutes.
  • Warm Moroccan Spice Rub: Cumin, smoked paprika, turmeric, and a hint of cinnamon create a fragrant, warm Mediterranean crust that pairs naturally with the sweetness of sweet potatoes.
  • Pomegranate & Fresh Mint Finish: Sprinkling tart, juicy pomegranate arils and torn mint over the hot roasted pan introduces bursts of fresh acidity that brighten every bite.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat Oven & Par-Cook Sweet Potatoes: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Place 1/2-inch diced sweet potatoes in a microwave-safe bowl with 1 tbsp water; cover with a plate and microwave on high for 3 minutes until fork-tender. Drain any remaining water.
  2. Season Chicken & Vegetables: In a large bowl, combine chicken thighs, par-cooked sweet potatoes, and red onion wedges. Drizzle with olive oil. Sprinkle with cumin, smoked paprika, coriander, turmeric, cinnamon, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Toss with hands until thoroughly coated in the fragrant spice rub.
  3. Arrange on Sheet Pan: Spread chicken and vegetables across a large rimmed baking sheet in a single layer. Make sure the sweet potatoes and onions are scattered evenly around the chicken thighs so everything roasts evenly.
  4. Roast & Broil: Roast at 425°F for 14 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F. Switch oven to HIGH BROIL for the final 2 minutes to blister the chicken skin and caramelize the sweet potato edges.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Remove sheet pan from oven. Drizzle the creamy garlic lemon yogurt across the hot chicken and sweet potatoes. Shower with ruby pomegranate arils, fresh chopped cilantro, and mint leaves. Serve straight from the pan with lemon wedges!

Elena’s 1/2-Inch Knife Uniformity Rule

Take 60 extra seconds to cut your sweet potatoes into uniform 1/2-inch dice! Uneven chunks mean tiny pieces burn while large ones stay hard in the center. Consistent sizing guarantees every single sweet potato cube turns creamy inside with golden caramelized edges in under 15 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this with chicken breasts?
Yes! Boneless skinless chicken breasts work well; cut them into large 2-inch chunks so they roast evenly with the sweet potatoes in 12–14 minutes.
Can I prep this the night before?
Yes, toss the chicken, potatoes, and spices together in an airtight container up to 24 hours in advance. When ready, spread on your sheet pan and roast!
What can I substitute for pomegranate seeds?
Dried cranberries, chopped dried apricots, or golden raisins provide a similar sweet-tart chewiness that complements Moroccan spices.
