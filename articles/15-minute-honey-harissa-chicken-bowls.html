15-Minute Sweet and Spicy Honey Harissa Chicken Bowls

Vibrant North African street flavors in 15 minutes: tender chicken thighs glazed in spicy rose harissa, honey, and lemon over steaming couscous with fresh herbs and cool garlic yogurt sauce.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute sweet and spicy honey harissa chicken bowls, cut 1.25 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs into bite-sized strips and season with salt, cumin, and coriander. Whisk 2.5 tbsp North African harissa paste with 2 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp olive oil, and juice of 1/2 lemon. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over high heat; sear chicken undisturbed for 3 minutes per side until caramelized and cooked through (165°F). Lower heat to medium, pour in the honey harissa glaze, and toss for 60 seconds until chicken is lacquered. Meanwhile, pour 1 cup boiling salted broth over 1 cup dry couscous; cover for 5 minutes and fluff with a fork. Assemble bowls with couscous, spicy glazed chicken, diced Persian cucumbers, pickled red onions, crumbled feta cheese, and a drizzle of garlic lemon yogurt.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Harissa Paste Bloom: Tossing the sweet-spicy harissa glaze into the skillet during the final minute caramelizes the natural sugars and blooms the chili peppers without scorching.
  • 5-Minute Steam Couscous: Moroccan couscous requires no stovetop boiling—steeping in boiling water off-heat produces fluffy, delicate grains in the exact time it takes to sear chicken.
  • Cooling Yogurt-Tahini Balance: A swirl of whole-milk Greek yogurt mixed with tahini and lemon provides a velvety, cooling fat barrier that balances the bold smoky heat of harissa.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Fluff the Couscous: Place 1 cup dry couscous in a heatproof bowl with 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tbsp olive oil. Pour 1 cup boiling chicken broth over the couscous, cover tightly with a plate, and let steam undisturbed for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
  2. Season Chicken: Pat chicken thigh strips dry with paper towels. Toss in a bowl with cumin, coriander, salt, and black pepper.
  3. High-Heat Sear: Heat 1.5 tbsp olive oil in a wide heavy skillet over high heat. Add chicken strips in a single layer and sear undisturbed for 3 minutes until deep golden brown. Flip and sear for another 2.5 minutes until cooked through (165°F).
  4. Glaze in Honey Harissa: Reduce heat to medium-low. In a small cup, stir harissa paste, honey, and lemon juice. Pour over the hot chicken in the skillet. Toss continuously for 60 to 90 seconds until a bubbling, sticky, fiery-red glaze coats every piece of chicken.
  5. Assemble Bowls: Divide fluffy couscous among 4 serving bowls. Top with generous scoops of the honey harissa chicken, diced cucumbers, pickled red onions, and crumbled feta. Drizzle with creamy garlic yogurt sauce and shower with torn fresh mint leaves!

Elena’s Sticky Glaze Heat Control

Honey burns quickly at high temperatures! Make sure to lower your stove burner to medium-low before pouring in the honey harissa mixture. You want the sauce to gently bubble and lacquer the seared chicken in 60 seconds rather than charring into bitterness.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I substitute for couscous?
Quinoa, warm jasmine rice, farro, or cauliflower rice all make fantastic grain-bowl bases with this recipe.
Can I use chicken breasts instead of thighs?
Yes! Boneless chicken breasts sliced into thin cutlets work well. Sear for 2.5 to 3 minutes per side to prevent drying out.
Is harissa extremely spicy?
Most jarred harissa pastes offer rich smokiness and moderate warmth rather than pure fiery burn. If you prefer mild heat, use 1.5 tbsp harissa and increase honey to 2.5 tbsp.
