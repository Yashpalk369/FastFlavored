15-Minute Creamy Garlic Butter Lemon Pepper Shrimp Pasta

Plump pink seared jumbo shrimp tossed with delicate angel hair pasta ribbons in a luscious garlic lemon pepper butter sauce in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute lemon pepper shrimp pasta, boil 8 oz angel hair pasta for 4 minutes (reserve 1/2 cup pasta water). Season 1 lb peeled jumbo shrimp with 1.5 tbsp lemon pepper seasoning and salt; sear in 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter for 2 minutes per side until pink; set aside. In the same skillet, melt 2 tbsp butter with 4 minced garlic cloves for 60 seconds. Pour in 1/3 cup heavy cream, 1/4 cup reserved pasta water, juice of 1 lemon, and 1/2 cup grated parmesan until glossy. Toss pasta and shrimp into the sauce for 60 seconds and garnish with fresh parsley.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Angel Hair 4-Minute Speed: Delicate angel hair pasta cooks in boiling water in 4 minutes flat, syncing perfectly with flash-seared jumbo shrimp.
  • Starchy Butter Emulsion: Combining hot starchy pasta cooking water with cold butter and heavy cream creates a silky pan emulsion that grips every pasta strand.
  • Zesty Lemon Pepper Crust: Coating shrimp in cracked black pepper and fresh lemon zest before searing infuses seafood with vibrant citrus heat.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil Angel Hair & Save Pasta Water: Bring a large pot of salted water to a rapid rolling boil. Drop in angel hair pasta and cook for exactly 3 to 4 minutes until al dente. Before draining, ladle out 1/2 cup of hot starchy pasta water. Drain pasta.
  2. Season & Flash-Sear Shrimp: Pat peeled shrimp completely dry with paper towels; toss with lemon pepper seasoning and a pinch of salt. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a wide skillet over high heat. Add shrimp in a single layer and sear for 2 minutes per side until pink and caramelized. Transfer shrimp to a plate.
  3. Sauté Garlic Butter Aromatics: Reduce heat to medium. Melt remaining 2 tbsp butter in the skillet drippings; add minced garlic and red pepper flakes. Sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant without browning.
  4. Build Velvety Lemon Cream Sauce: Pour in heavy cream, 1/4 cup of the reserved hot pasta water, lemon juice, and lemon zest. Whisk continuously for 60 seconds as the sauce bubbles into a silky, emulsified glaze. Stir in grated parmesan cheese until melted.
  5. Toss Pasta & Shrimp: Add the drained angel hair pasta and seared shrimp back into the skillet. Toss gently with tongs for 60 seconds until pasta is glistening in garlic lemon butter sauce. Shower with chopped fresh parsley and extra cracked black pepper. Serve immediately with lemon wedges!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Angel hair pasta continues cooking in the residual heat of the skillet! Pull it out of the boiling water 60 seconds BEFORE the box instructions say it is done (around the 3-minute mark). It will finish absorbing the luxurious garlic lemon butter sauce in the skillet without turning into mush.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen shrimp?
Yes! Thaw frozen shrimp completely in cold water for 15 minutes, peel, and squeeze dry with clean paper towels before seasoning.
Can I make this dairy-free?
Yes! Replace butter with extra olive oil or vegan butter, and substitute heavy cream with unsweetened canned coconut milk or cashew cream.
How do I keep angel hair from clumping?
Immediately toss hot drained angel hair with the sauce and a splash of reserved pasta water. The starchy water and fats lubricate the thin noodles so they stay silky.
