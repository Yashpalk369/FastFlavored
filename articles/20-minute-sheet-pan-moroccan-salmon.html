20-Minute Sheet-Pan Moroccan Spiced Salmon and Asparagus

Thick center-cut salmon fillets and green asparagus rubbed in Moroccan chermoula spices, roasted on one pan and drizzled with lemon tahini sauce in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan Moroccan salmon, mix 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 3 minced garlic cloves, juice of 1 lemon, salt, and pepper. Rub spice paste over 4 center-cut salmon fillets. Place salmon, 1 lb trimmed asparagus, and 1 cup cherry tomatoes on a rimmed baking sheet; drizzle vegetables with olive oil. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 12–14 minutes until salmon flakes easily. Drizzle with a 60-second lemon tahini herb sauce (whisk 1/4 cup tahini, 2 tbsp warm water, 1 tbsp lemon juice, and chopped fresh dill).

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Chermoula Spice Crust: Rubbing salmon fillets in cumin, smoked paprika, turmeric, and garlic creates an aromatic Moroccan crust that seals in omega-3 moisture.
  • Nutty Lemon Tahini Drizzle: Creamy toasted sesame tahini blended with fresh lemon juice and dill cuts through rich salmon oils without dairy.
  • Perfect Roasting Synchronization: Thick center-cut salmon fillets and fresh asparagus spears cook to tender perfection in the exact same 13-minute window at 425°F.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat & Season Vegetables: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper. Place trimmed asparagus spears and cherry tomatoes on the baking sheet, drizzle with 1 tbsp olive oil, and toss with a pinch of salt and pepper. Push to sides of pan.
  2. Rub Moroccan Chermoula Paste on Salmon: In a small bowl, mix remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, coriander, turmeric, cayenne, salt, and half the lemon juice into a fragrant spice paste. Place salmon fillets skin-side down in the center of the sheet pan and spread the spice paste generously across the top of each fillet.
  3. Roast to Flaky Perfection: Roast at 425°F for 12 to 14 minutes until salmon flakes tenderly with a fork (internal temperature 130°F for medium) and asparagus is tender-crisp.
  4. Whisk 60-Second Lemon Tahini Sauce: While salmon roasts, whisk tahini paste, remaining lemon juice, warm water, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl until smooth, creamy, and pourable. Stir in fresh chopped dill.
  5. Drizzle & Serve: Pull baking sheet from oven. Drizzle the velvety lemon tahini sauce generously across the hot salmon and roasted asparagus. Scatter fresh cilantro and dill leaves on top, and serve immediately with warm pita or couscous!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: When whisking tahini with cold lemon juice, it will initially seize and turn paste-like—don't panic! Simply whisk in 1 to 2 tablespoons of warm water, and within 30 seconds it will emulsify into a silky, pourable, restaurant-grade cream sauce.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use skinless salmon fillets?
Yes! Skinless fillets work identically. Just make sure your parchment paper is lightly oiled so they release cleanly.
Can I make this with chicken or white fish?
Yes! Thick cod fillets or boneless chicken thighs roast beautifully with this Moroccan chermoula spice blend.
How do I store and reheat leftovers?
Store salmon and vegetables in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Reheat gently in a 350°F oven or covered skillet so the fish stays tender.
