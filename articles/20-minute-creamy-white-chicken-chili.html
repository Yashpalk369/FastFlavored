Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy white chicken chili, sauté diced onions and minced garlic in olive oil with cumin, chili powder, and oregano. Add 3 cups chicken broth, 2 cans cannellini beans (mash half a can for body), 1 can green chiles, sweet corn, and 3 cups shredded chicken. Simmer for 8 minutes, then stir in 4 oz softened cream cheese and fresh lime juice until velvety and rich.

Why This Chili Is Pure Weeknight Gold

  • Rotisserie Chicken Shortcut: Using shredded rotisserie chicken reduces the simmer time from 2 hours to just 15 minutes without sacrificing flavor.
  • No Heavy Flour Roux: The combination of mashed cannellini bean starch and melted cream cheese gives the chili natural, luxurious thickness.
  • High Fiber & Protein: Packed with 35g of lean protein and 7g of prebiotic dietary fiber per bowl.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sauté Aromatics: In a Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat. Sauté diced onion 3 minutes. Stir in garlic, cumin, chili powder, and oregano; cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Add Base & Mash: Pour in broth, green chiles, corn, shredded chicken, and 1.5 cans beans. Mash the remaining 1/2 can beans on a plate with a fork and stir in.
  3. Simmer: Bring soup to a gentle simmer for 8 minutes to meld flavors.
  4. Melt Cream Cheese: Turn heat to medium-low. Add cubed cream cheese; whisk gently for 2–3 minutes until melted and creamy.
  5. Season & Serve: Stir in fresh lime juice and season with salt and pepper. Top with avocado, cheese, and cilantro!

Elena’s Mashed-Bean Secret

The Starch Mash Trick: Instead of using heavy flour or cornstarch, always mash half a can of your cannellini beans with a fork before adding them to the pot! The released bean starch naturally emulsifies the chicken broth and cream cheese into an ultra-velvety texture without altering the chili's authentic Southwestern flavor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you thicken white chicken chili without heavy flour?
Two simple tricks: mash half a can of cannellini beans with a fork before stirring them into the pot, and melt cubed cream cheese directly into the broth. The natural bean starch combined with cream cheese creates a luscious, velvety body with zero flour.
Can I use raw chicken breasts instead of cooked shredded chicken?
Yes! Submerge two boneless raw chicken breasts directly in the simmering broth. Simmer covered for 12–14 minutes until cooked through (165°F), transfer to a plate, shred with two forks, and return to the pot with the cream cheese.
Does white chicken chili freeze well?
If freezing, omit the cream cheese before freezing, as dairy can separate when thawed. Freeze the chicken and bean chili for up to 3 months, then melt the cream cheese into the pot when reheating on the stove.
