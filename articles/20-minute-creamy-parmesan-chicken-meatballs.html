20-Minute Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Meatball Skillet

Tender golden pan-seared ground chicken meatballs simmered in a luscious garlic parmesan cream sauce, topped with melted mozzarella and fresh basil in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy chicken meatballs, mix 1 lb ground chicken with 1/3 cup panko, 1/4 cup grated parmesan, 1 egg, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper; roll into 12 golf-ball-sized meatballs. Sear meatballs in 1.5 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat for 6 minutes, turning until golden brown on all sides. Pour in 3/4 cup heavy cream, 1/3 cup chicken broth, 2 minced garlic cloves, and 1/2 cup grated parmesan. Simmer 5 minutes until sauce thickens and meatballs reach 165°F. Top with 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella, cover to melt for 2 minutes, and garnish with basil.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Panko Moisture Sponge: Mixing panko breadcrumbs and an egg into lean ground chicken locks in internal juices so the meatballs stay fluffy and tender.
  • Skillet Pan Fond Integration: Simmering heavy cream and chicken broth directly in the meatball drippings deglazes the pan for rich homemade flavor.
  • Melted Mozzarella Blanket: Covering the skillet for the final 2 minutes melts shredded mozzarella over the meatballs into a gooey Italian bistro presentation.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix & Shape Chicken Meatballs: In a medium bowl, combine ground chicken, panko, 1/4 cup parmesan, beaten egg, 2 minced garlic cloves, Italian seasoning, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Gently mix with hands until combined (do not overwork). Roll into 12 equal-sized balls (about 1.5 inches each).
  2. Sear to Golden Crust: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add meatballs and sear for 5 to 6 minutes, gently turning with tongs every 90 seconds until golden-brown crust forms on all sides. (Meatballs do not need to be cooked through yet).
  3. Build Garlic Parmesan Cream: Reduce skillet heat to medium. Scatter the remaining 2 minced garlic cloves around the meatballs for 30 seconds. Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream, scraping up the flavorful fond from the pan bottom. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Simmer Meatballs to Perfection: Stir in 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese. Simmer uncovered for 4 to 5 minutes, spooning sauce over meatballs frequently, until the sauce thickens and meatballs reach an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).
  5. Melt Mozzarella & Garnish: Scatter shredded mozzarella cheese evenly over the bubbling skillet. Cover with a lid for 90 seconds until the cheese is gloriously melted and bubbling. Garnish with chopped fresh basil and cracked pepper. Serve immediately over pasta, rice, or with warm crusty bread!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Use a light hand when mixing ground chicken! Overworking ground poultry compresses the meat proteins and results in dense, rubbery meatballs. Gently folding the ingredients just until combined guarantees light, pillowy, melt-in-your-mouth meatballs every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make these in the air fryer?
Yes! Air fry shaped meatballs at 380°F for 8–10 minutes. While they air fry, simmer the cream sauce in a skillet, then toss the cooked meatballs in to coat.
Can I freeze chicken meatballs?
Yes! Freeze cooked or uncooked meatballs on a parchment-lined sheet pan for 2 hours, then transfer to a freezer bag for up to 3 months.
What can I serve with creamy chicken meatballs?
They are incredible served over buttered fettuccine, mashed potatoes, cauliflower mash, polenta, or simply scooped up with warm garlic bread.
