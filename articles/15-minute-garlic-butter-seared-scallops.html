15-Minute Garlic Chili Butter Seared Sea Scallops

Plump caramelized jumbo sea scallops pan-seared golden in a cast-iron skillet, basted in foaming garlic chili herb butter and fresh lemon in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute garlic chili butter seared scallops, remove the side muscle and pat 1 lb jumbo dry sea scallops bone-dry with paper towels; season with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp avocado oil in a cast-iron skillet over smoking high heat. Sear scallops undisturbed for 2 minutes until a deep golden caramelized crust forms, then flip. Add 3 tbsp unsalted butter, 4 minced garlic cloves, 1/4 tsp red chili flakes, and 1 sprig fresh thyme. Baste bubbling butter over scallops for 90 seconds until opaque (125°F). Finish with fresh lemon juice and minced chives.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Bone-Dry Surface Sear: Drying scallops thoroughly with paper towels removes moisture so proteins brown immediately instead of boiling in steam.
  • Cast-Iron Thermal Mass: Heavy cast iron holds screaming heat when cold scallops hit the pan, creating a 5-star restaurant crust in 120 seconds.
  • Arrosé Butter Basting: Spoon-basting foaming garlic butter cook-finishes the top of the scallops while bathing them in nutty aromatics.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep & Dry Scallops: Inspect scallops and peel off the small tough rectangular side muscle on each one (it pulls right off). Place scallops on a paper towel-lined plate and firmly press with additional paper towels until the exterior is completely bone-dry. Season both flat sides generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Get Skillet Smoking Hot: Add 1 tbsp avocado oil to a heavy 10- or 12-inch cast-iron skillet over high heat. Wait until the oil is shimmering and just beginning to show wisps of smoke.
  3. Sear Undisturbed for 2 Minutes: Place scallops in the skillet clockwise, leaving at least 1 inch of space between each one. Press each scallop down gently with fingertips to ensure full surface contact. Cook undisturbed for exactly 2 minutes without moving, checking for a deep golden-brown crust.
  4. Flip & Baste with Garlic Butter: Carefully flip scallops using a thin metal spatula. Immediately drop 3 tbsp butter, minced garlic, red chili flakes, and thyme sprig into the skillet. As butter foams, tilt pan slightly and spoon hot foaming garlic butter over the scallops continuously for 90 seconds until opaque and barely firm to the touch.
  5. Finish & Serve Immediately: Remove skillet from heat. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the bubbling butter and scatter minced chives and lemon zest. Transfer scallops immediately to warm plates and spoon delicious garlic pan butter on top. Serve right away!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Always buy 'dry-packed' scallops from your fishmonger! 'Wet' scallops are soaked in a chemical phosphate bath that forces them to absorb water weight; when cooked, that water floods your pan, boiling the scallops into rubbery pucks with zero crust. Dry scallops sear into golden candy in 120 seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know when scallops are cooked?
Scallops are done when the sides have turned from translucent to opaque white and feel gently springy when pressed with your finger (around 125°F–130°F internal temp). Overcooking makes them rubbery!
What should I serve with garlic butter scallops?
They pair divinely with creamy parmesan risotto, garlic mashed potatoes, angel hair aglio e olio, or crusty warm sourdough baguettes.
Can I freeze leftover cooked scallops?
Never freeze cooked scallops as their delicate cellular structure breaks down, turning mushy upon thawing. Eat them fresh within 24 hours.
