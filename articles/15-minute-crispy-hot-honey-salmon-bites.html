Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy hot honey salmon bites, cut skinless salmon into 1-inch cubes and toss with olive oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Air fry at 400°F (200°C) in a single uncrowded layer for 7 minutes until crispy on the edges. Toss immediately in a warm glaze of hot honey, melted butter, grated garlic, and fresh lime juice for a glossy, sweet-spicy crunch.

Why Salmon Bites Outshine Whole Fillets

  • 7-Minute Cook Time: Bite-sized cubes cook in less than half the time of large salmon fillets with zero dry spots.
  • Maximized Sticky Glaze: Every single morsel is coated on all six sides with sweet, spicy garlic butter glaze.
  • Versatile Bowl Builder: Spoon over white rice, quinoa, or fresh mixed greens with avocado and spicy mayo for restaurant-worthy bowls.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season Salmon: Pat salmon cubes dry. Toss with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  2. Air Fry: Preheat air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Air fry salmon cubes in a single layer for 7 minutes until crispy and browned.
  3. Whisk Glaze: Whisk hot honey, melted butter, grated garlic, and lime juice in a small bowl until smooth.
  4. Toss & Coat: Transfer crispy salmon into a bowl. Pour hot honey glaze over top and toss gently to coat.
  5. Serve: Garnish with sesame seeds and scallions. Serve immediately over warm rice bowls with avocado and lime!

Elena’s Hot Honey Emulsion Trick

Balance Heat with Melted Butter & Lime: Raw honey straight from the bottle is too thick and sticky to evenly coat delicate fish. Whisking warm melted butter and fresh lime juice directly into the hot honey creates an irresistible sweet, tangy, and spicy emulsion that clings to the crispy salmon crust like silk!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make hot honey salmon bites in the oven if I don't have an air fryer?
Yes! Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Place seasoned salmon bites on a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake for 8 minutes, then switch to high broil for 2 minutes to get that signature crispy exterior before tossing in the glaze.
How do you prevent salmon from sticking to the air fryer basket?
Preheat your air fryer for 3 minutes and lightly brush or spray the basket with avocado oil. Tossing the salmon cubes in 1.5 tablespoons of oil also ensures they release effortlessly with a golden crust.
