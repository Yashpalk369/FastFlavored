15-Minute Crispy Teriyaki Glazed Tofu Rice Bowls

Restaurant-crispy tofu in 15 minutes: extra-firm tofu cubes tossed in cornstarch, pan-fried to shatteringly crisp perfection, and glazed in homemade honey ginger teriyaki sauce over rice.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy teriyaki tofu rice bowls, press 1 block (14 oz) extra-firm tofu firmly in paper towels to extract moisture, cut into 1-inch cubes, and toss with 2 tbsp cornstarch and 1/2 tsp salt until powdery and coated. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add tofu cubes in a single layer; pan-fry for 6 to 7 minutes, turning every 2 minutes until all sides are golden brown and crunchy. In a small cup, whisk 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp mirin, 2 tbsp brown sugar (or maple syrup), 1 minced garlic clove, and 1 tsp grated ginger. Pour the teriyaki sauce over the hot crispy tofu; toss for 60 seconds as the sauce bubbles into a thick, glossy lacquer. Spoon over warm sushi rice with steamed edamame, sliced avocado, and sesame seeds.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Cornstarch Protective Crust: Dredging dry tofu cubes in cornstarch creates a barrier that locks in moisture while frying into a shatteringly crisp exterior shell.
  • Super-Firm Tofu Selection: Selecting high-protein extra-firm or vacuum-packed super-firm tofu skips 30 minutes of heavy pressing and holds clean cube shapes in the skillet.
  • Simmering Teriyaki Caramelization: Pouring the mirin-soy-ginger sauce into the hot skillet glazes the crispy crust instantly without making the tofu soggy.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Dry & Coat Tofu: Wrap the tofu block in several layers of paper towels and press firmly with your palms to extract surface water. Cut into uniform 1-inch cubes. Place in a bowl, sprinkle with cornstarch and a pinch of salt, and toss gently until all sides are evenly coated with a dry powdery layer.
  2. Pan-Fry to Shattering Crispness: Heat oil in a large cast iron or non-stick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add tofu cubes in a single layer with space between each piece. Cook for 6 to 7 minutes, turning with tongs every 2 minutes so each facet becomes deeply golden and crackly.
  3. Whisk Teriyaki Glaze: In a measuring cup or small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, mirin, brown sugar, water, minced garlic, and grated ginger until sugar dissolves.
  4. Glaze the Crispy Tofu: Pour the teriyaki sauce directly over the hot sizzling tofu in the skillet. Toss continuously with a spatula for 60 to 90 seconds as the sauce bubbles violently, reduces, and forms a thick, sticky, amber lacquer coating every cube.
  5. Assemble Bowls: Divide warm rice into four bowls. Top with generous heaps of sticky glazed tofu, steamed edamame, sliced avocado, and shaved radishes. Drizzle with extra pan sauce and shower with toasted sesame seeds and scallions!

Elena’s Cornstarch Tossing Trick

Toss your tofu cubes in cornstarch inside a large sealable container or gallon ziplock bag! Shaking the bag gently aerates the cubes and spreads an ultra-thin, microscopic film of starch over every single millimeter of tofu, guaranteeing 100% crunch without clumping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this in the air fryer?
Yes! Air fry cornstarch-coated tofu at 400°F for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway until crisp. Simmer the teriyaki glaze in a small skillet and toss the hot air-fried tofu right in.
Is this recipe gluten-free?
To make it 100% gluten-free, substitute tamari or gluten-free soy sauce for traditional soy sauce.
How do I store leftovers?
Store glazed tofu in an airtight container for up to 4 days. Reheat in a dry skillet or toaster oven for 3 minutes to bring back the crisp outer texture.
