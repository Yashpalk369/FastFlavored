15-Minute Creamy Coconut Lime Garlic Shrimp Rice Noodle Bowls

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute creamy coconut lime shrimp noodle bowls, soak 8 oz thin rice vermicelli noodles in boiling water for 4 minutes, then drain and rinse with cold water. Pat 1 lb raw peeled jumbo shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp coconut or avocado oil in a deep skillet over high heat; sear shrimp for 90 seconds per side until pink and curled, then transfer to a plate. Reduce heat to medium, add 1 tbsp oil, 4 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger, and 1 thinly sliced red chili; sauté for 45 seconds. Pour in 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk, 1.5 tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce), 1 tbsp brown sugar, and zest and juice of 1 large lime. Simmer for 3 minutes until velvety. Return shrimp and noodles to the skillet to warm through. Serve topped with crushed peanuts, fresh cilantro, and shredded purple cabbage.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Vermicelli Steep Shortcut: Thin rice vermicelli noodles require no stove boiling—simply submerge in hot tap or kettle water for 4 minutes to achieve tender, springy noodles.
  • Flash-Sear Shrimp Timing: Jumbo shrimp cook in just 3 minutes total; removing them from the skillet while making the coconut broth prevents rubbery overcooking.
  • Lime Zest Flavor Secret: Fresh lime juice adds acidity, but the zest contains aromatic citrus oils that infuse authentic Southeast Asian vibrancy into rich coconut milk.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Soak Rice Noodles: Place rice vermicelli noodles in a large heatproof bowl. Pour boiling water over the noodles until fully submerged. Let soak for 4 minutes until tender. Drain in a colander, rinse briefly with cold water to stop cooking, and set aside.
  2. Flash-Sear Shrimp: Pat peeled shrimp completely dry with paper towels. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp coconut oil in a wide deep skillet over high heat until shimmering. Add shrimp in a single layer and sear for 90 seconds per side until pink, curled, and slightly caramelized. Transfer shrimp to a plate.
  3. Sauté Aromatics: Reduce skillet heat to medium. Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and sliced red chilies. Sauté for 45 to 60 seconds until fragrant and sizzling without burning.
  4. Simmer Coconut Lime Broth: Pour in the full-fat coconut milk, fish sauce, brown sugar, lime zest, and lime juice. Whisk gently and bring to a bubbling simmer over medium heat for 3 minutes until smooth and fragrant.
  5. Toss & Serve: Add the drained rice noodles and seared shrimp into the bubbling coconut broth. Toss gently with tongs for 60 seconds until heated through. Divide into wide bowls and top with shredded purple cabbage, chopped peanuts, fresh cilantro, and lime wedges!

Elena’s Cold Rinse Noodle Habit

Always rinse cooked rice noodles under cold running water immediately after draining! Rinsing washes away excess surface starches that cause rice noodles to turn mushy and clump into a solid brick. When tossed into the hot coconut broth, the clean individual strands absorb the broth cleanly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this vegetarian / vegan?
Yes! Swap the shrimp for crispy pan-seared cubed tofu or edamame, and substitute soy sauce or vegetarian stir-fry sauce for fish sauce.
Can I use light coconut milk?
You can, but light coconut milk produces a much thinner, watery broth. Full-fat canned coconut milk creates that luxurious, creamy restaurant texture.
How do I store leftovers?
Store leftover shrimp and coconut broth separately from the rice noodles if possible, as noodles will continue absorbing liquid. Keep in the fridge for up to 2 days.
