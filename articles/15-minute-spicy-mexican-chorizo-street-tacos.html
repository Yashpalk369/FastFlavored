15-Minute Sizzling Mexican Pork Chorizo Street Tacos

Authentic street cart flavor in 15 minutes: crispy crumbly Mexican pork chorizo crisped in a hot skillet, served in doubled corn tortillas with fresh cilantro, onions, and crumbled cotija.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute Mexican pork chorizo street tacos, remove casings from 1 lb fresh Mexican pork chorizo and add to a dry skillet over medium-high heat. Break apart with a wooden spoon and fry for 7 to 8 minutes until deeply browned, crispy at the edges, and fully rendered (drain excess rendered oil if needed). While chorizo crisps, warm 8 small corn tortillas directly over an open gas burner for 15 seconds per side until lightly charred and pliable. Double up the tortillas (two per taco), spoon in generous heaps of sizzling chorizo, and top with finely diced white onion, chopped fresh cilantro, crumbled salty cotija cheese, and sliced radishes. Serve immediately with fresh lime wedges.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Raw Mexican vs. Cured Spanish Chorizo: Always buy raw fresh Mexican pork chorizo (seasoned with vinegar, ancho, and paprika); it renders into crispy crumbly taco meat unlike hard cured Spanish chorizo.
  • Rendered Fat Browning: Chorizo contains rich pork fat that crisps the seasoned meat in its own oils—no extra cooking oil is required in the skillet.
  • Raw Onion & Lime Acidity Cut: Finely diced sharp white onion and fresh lime juice cut cleanly through the rich, spiced pork fats for street-cart balance.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Crisp the Chorizo: Place a large cast iron or heavy stainless skillet over medium-high heat. Add raw pork chorizo and break it apart with a wooden spoon or potato masher. Cook for 7 to 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the meat is deeply browned, rendered, and develops dark crispy caramelized bits. (Tilt pan and spoon out excess grease if desired).
  2. Char Corn Tortillas: While chorizo cooks, char the corn tortillas directly over a low open gas flame for 15 seconds per side until pliable with blistered spots (or warm in a dry hot skillet).
  3. Double Stack Tortillas: Stack two warm tortillas on top of each other for each taco (8 double-stacked taco bases total) on a wooden platter or plates.
  4. Fill with Sizzling Chorizo: Spoon generous portions of the hot crispy chorizo down the center of each doubled tortilla.
  5. Top & Serve: Shower each taco with finely diced white onion, chopped cilantro, crumbled cotija cheese, and crisp radish coins. Squeeze fresh lime juice generously over the tacos and serve piping hot!

Elena’s Double Tortilla Street Rule

Authentic taquerias always serve street tacos with two tortillas stacked together! Sizzling chorizo releases flavorful chili oils that will soak through and split a single corn tortilla. Doubling them up catches all the savory juices so your taco stays sturdy and delicious to the very last bite.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add eggs to this?
Yes! Chorizo con huevos (chorizo with scrambled eggs) makes an extraordinary 15-minute breakfast-for-dinner taco.
Where do I find cotija cheese?
Cotija is found in the refrigerated specialty cheese or Hispanic foods section. If unavailable, crumbled salty feta cheese makes a wonderful substitute.
How do I store leftover chorizo?
Store cooked chorizo in an airtight container for up to 5 days in the fridge. Reheat in a dry skillet over medium heat for 2 minutes to restore the crispy edges.
