20-Minute Creamy Garlic Herb Salmon Bites with Orzo Skillet

Tender golden seared salmon bites nestled in creamy, risotto-style garlic herb parmesan orzo with fresh dill and lemon wheels in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute salmon bites with garlic herb orzo, cut 1.25 lbs skinless salmon into 1-inch cubes, season with salt, garlic powder, and paprika, and sear in 1 tbsp olive oil for 2 minutes per side until golden; set aside. In the same skillet, melt 1 tbsp butter, toast 1 cup dried orzo with 3 minced garlic cloves for 2 minutes, then pour in 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth. Simmer covered for 9 minutes until liquid is absorbed and orzo is tender. Stir in 1/3 cup heavy cream, 1/2 cup grated parmesan, 2 tbsp fresh dill, and juice of 1/2 lemon. Nestle salmon bites on top to warm through.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Salmon Bite Flash Sear: Cubing salmon into 1-inch pieces develops a gorgeous golden crust in just 4 total minutes while retaining a buttery melt-in-your-mouth interior.
  • Toasted Orzo Depth: Toasting dry orzo in butter and garlic before adding broth creates a nutty flavor profile and locks starch for a creamy risotto texture.
  • Single-Skillet Convenience: Cooking the pasta directly in seasoned broth eliminates boiling separate water pots and captures all the savory fond from the salmon.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season & Sear Salmon Bites: Pat salmon cubes dry with paper towels; toss gently with olive oil, salt, black pepper, and paprika. Heat a large cast-iron or heavy stainless skillet over medium-high heat. Add salmon cubes in a single layer and sear undisturbed for 2 minutes until golden. Flip and sear 90 seconds more until medium. Transfer salmon to a plate.
  2. Toast Orzo in Garlic Butter: Melt 1 tbsp butter in the same hot skillet over medium heat. Add dry orzo and minced garlic; toast, stirring constantly, for 2 minutes until pasta is lightly golden and aromatic.
  3. Simmer Orzo to Creamy Al Dente: Pour in chicken broth and scrape up any browned salmon bits from the bottom. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and simmer gently for 8 to 9 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until liquid is mostly absorbed.
  4. Fold in Cream, Parmesan & Herbs: Remove lid. Stir in heavy cream, grated parmesan, remaining 1 tbsp butter, fresh dill, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Stir vigorously for 60 seconds until a glossy, creamy risotto-like sauce forms.
  5. Nestle Salmon & Garnish: Gently place seared salmon bites on top of the creamy orzo. Warm through for 60 seconds. Garnish with fresh dill sprigs, cracked black pepper, and lemon wheels. Serve immediately from the skillet!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Do not stir the salmon bites while they first hit the skillet! Giving them 2 full minutes of undisturbed contact with hot cast iron creates a deep golden crust that seals in natural juices so they don't flake apart when resting on the creamy orzo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen salmon?
Yes! Thaw salmon completely in the refrigerator or in a cold water bath, and dry very thoroughly with paper towels before cutting into cubes.
Can I substitute rice for orzo?
Orzo is pasta shaped like rice. If substituting real rice, use white jasmine or arborio rice, but increase chicken broth to 2.25 cups and simmer for 15–18 minutes.
How do I store and reheat leftovers?
Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet over low heat with 2 tablespoons of water or broth to loosen the creamy orzo.
