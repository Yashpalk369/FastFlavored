20-Minute Sheet-Pan Crispy Lemon Feta Greek Chicken Thighs

Juicy caramelized Greek chicken thighs roasted on one pan with blistered bell peppers, kalamata olives, red onions, and golden baked feta cheese blocks in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan lemon feta chicken, toss 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs with 2 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 3 minced garlic cloves, 1.5 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp paprika, salt, and pepper. Spread on a rimmed baking sheet alongside 1 sliced bell pepper, 1 sliced red onion, 1 cup cherry tomatoes, 1/2 cup pitted kalamata olives, and 6 oz Greek feta cheese sliced into thick slabs. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 14 minutes, then broil on High for 2 minutes until chicken edges and feta tops are blistered and charred. Serve with warm pita.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Warm Caramelized Feta Slabs: Baking thick slabs of Greek feta directly on the pan browns the exterior while creating a warm, spreadable, creamy interior.
  • High-Heat Mediterranean Roast: Roasting at 425°F blisters cherry tomatoes into natural sauce and chars the peppers and onions simultaneously with the chicken.
  • Lemon Oregano Pan Pan Juices: The rendered chicken juices combine with lemon, olive oil, and melted feta at the bottom of the pan to create an instant dressing for pita.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat & Season Chicken: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). In a large bowl, whisk 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, minced garlic, dried oregano, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Add chicken thigh pieces and toss until coated in the fragrant marinade.
  2. Arrange on Sheet Pan: Spread marinated chicken chunks across a rimmed baking sheet. Scatter bell pepper strips, red onion wedges, whole cherry tomatoes, and Kalamata olives around the chicken. Drizzle vegetables with remaining 1/2 tbsp olive oil.
  3. Add Feta Slabs: Nestle the thick slabs of Greek feta cheese into open spaces on the baking sheet, seasoning feta tops with a sprinkle of dried oregano and black pepper.
  4. Roast & Char-Broil: Roast at 425°F for 13 to 14 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F and tomatoes begin bursting. Switch oven to High Broil for the final 2 minutes until chicken edges and feta surfaces develop golden-brown charred blisters.
  5. Serve & Enjoy: Remove from oven. Squeeze extra fresh lemon juice over the hot sheet pan, sprinkle with fresh oregano, and serve directly from the pan with warm pita triangles and tzatziki!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Always buy block feta preserved in brine and cut it into thick slabs yourself! Pre-crumbled feta from tubs is coated in cellulose anti-caking powder that prevents caramelization and dries into chalky pebbles instead of softening into a warm, creamy, spreadable delicacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use bone-in chicken thighs?
Bone-in chicken thighs require 30–35 minutes to roast, which will overcook the vegetables and feta. Stick with boneless skinless thighs for this 20-minute meal.
What can I do with the pan juices?
The lemony chicken drippings, olive oil, and roasted tomato juices at the bottom of the pan are liquid gold! Mop them up with warm pita bread or spoon over steamed rice.
Can I add potatoes?
Raw potatoes take too long for a 20-minute roast. If you want potatoes, use pre-cooked leftover boiled baby potatoes or canned new potatoes quartered.
