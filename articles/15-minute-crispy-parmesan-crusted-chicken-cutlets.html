15-Minute Crispy Parmesan Crusted Chicken Cutlets with Lemon Arugula

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy parmesan crusted chicken cutlets, slice 2 large chicken breasts horizontally into 4 thin cutlets (approx. 1/3-inch thick). Set up two shallow dishes: one with 1 beaten egg, and the second with 1 cup panko breadcrumbs mixed with 1/2 cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Dip each cutlet in egg, then press firmly into the parmesan panko mixture until heavily crusted. Heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Fry cutlets for 3 minutes per side until deep golden brown and crunchy (165°F). Transfer to a wire rack, shower with flaky sea salt, and serve with charred lemon wedges and a lemon-tossed baby arugula salad.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • 50/50 Panko-Parmesan Crust Ratio: Blending equal parts airy Japanese panko with microplaned Parmigiano-Reggiano creates a shatteringly crisp crust that browns without burning.
  • Wire Cooling Rack Elevation: Never rest fried cutlets on a flat paper plate; resting them on a raised wire cooling rack allows steam to escape so the bottom crust stays crunch-crisp.
  • Peppery Arugula Lemon Foil: Tossing baby arugula with fresh lemon juice and olive oil provides an acidic, peppery contrast that cuts through rich pan-fried poultry.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep & Bread Cutlets: Pat thin chicken cutlets dry with paper towels. In shallow bowl one, beat egg with 1 tbsp water. In shallow bowl two, combine panko, grated parmesan, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Dredge each cutlet in egg, then press firmly into the parmesan panko mixture to coat both sides heavily.
  2. Shallow Pan-Fry to Golden Crisp: Heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Carefully lay cutlets into the hot oil (work in two batches if needed so pan isn't crowded). Fry undisturbed for 3 minutes until the bottom crust is deep golden brown and crunchy.
  3. Flip & Finish: Carefully flip cutlets with tongs and fry for another 2.5 to 3 minutes until golden and cooked through (165°F). Transfer immediately to a wire cooling rack and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
  4. Toss Arugula Salad: In a medium bowl, toss fresh baby arugula with 1 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1/2 lemon, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Plate & Serve: Slice crispy cutlets on a bias. Serve hot alongside the bright lemon arugula salad, showered with fresh chopped parsley and charred lemon wedges for squeezing!

Elena’s Firm Press Breading Rule

Don't just gently dust your chicken—use the flat palms of your hands to press the panko and parmesan crumbs firmly into the meat! Compacting the crumbs into the egg wash locks the breading to the cutlet so not a single golden flake falls off when you flip in the skillet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this in the air fryer?
Yes! Spray breaded cutlets generously with olive oil spray and air fry at 400°F for 8–9 minutes, flipping halfway until golden and crunchy.
How do I keep chicken cutlets warm while cooking batches?
Keep fried cutlets on a wire rack on a baking sheet in a 200°F oven; they will stay piping hot and crunchy for up to 30 minutes!
Can I use pork or veal?
Yes! Thin pork cutlets or veal scaloppine make classic Italian schnitzel/Milanese variations with identical cooking times.
