15-Minute Crispy Buffalo Cauliflower Tacos with Lime Crema

Crispy pan-seared buffalo spiced cauliflower florets nestled in warm charred corn tortillas, topped with crunchy shredded red cabbage slaw, cotija cheese, and cool zesty lime crema in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy buffalo cauliflower tacos, toss 4 cups small bite-sized cauliflower florets with 2 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and a pinch of salt. Pan-sear in 2 tbsp avocado oil in a wide skillet over high heat for 7–8 minutes until deeply browned and tender-crisp. Pour in 1/3 cup buffalo hot sauce and 1 tbsp melted butter, tossing for 60 seconds until glazed. Divide into 6 charred corn tortillas and top with shredded red cabbage, crumbled cotija, and a drizzle of lime sour cream.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • High-Heat Skillet Char: Small cauliflower florets cut to uniform bite sizes sear deeply on flat sides in 7 minutes, mimicking crispy wings without deep-frying.
  • Cornstarch Starch Shield: Dusting dry florets with cornstarch creates a crackly outer crust that holds buffalo glaze without getting mushy.
  • Tangy Lime Crema Counterbalance: Sour cream whisked with fresh lime juice and garlic cools down the spicy cayenne kick of Frank's RedHot buffalo sauce.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep & Dust Cauliflower: In a large bowl, toss the trimmed cauliflower florets with cornstarch, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and salt until evenly coated in a dry spice film.
  2. Sear to Blistered Crisp: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wide cast-iron or heavy stainless skillet over high heat. Add cauliflower in a single layer and leave undisturbed for 3–4 minutes until bottoms are deeply golden brown and blistered. Flip and sauté 3–4 more minutes until tender-crisp.
  3. Glaze in Buffalo Butter: Whisk buffalo hot sauce and melted butter together; pour directly into the hot skillet. Toss vigorously for 60 seconds until sauce bubbles, thickens, and lacquers every floret. Remove from heat.
  4. Whisk Zesty Lime Crema: In a small ramekin, whisk sour cream, lime juice, lime zest, and a pinch of salt until smooth and drizzle-ready.
  5. Char Tortillas & Assemble: Quickly warm corn tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over gas stove burner until blistered. Fill each tortilla with crunchy shredded purple cabbage, heaps of spicy buffalo cauliflower, a drizzle of lime crema, crumbled cotija, and fresh cilantro. Serve with extra lime wedges!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Keep the cauliflower pieces small—think the size of large grapes. Smaller florets cook all the way through to tender-crisp in 7 minutes flat while providing double the surface area for blistering char and spicy buffalo glaze.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make these dairy-free or vegan?
Yes! Substitute the melted butter with vegan butter (or coconut oil), and use dairy-free coconut or almond sour cream for the lime crema.
Can I use flour tortillas instead of corn?
Absolutely! Small flour street taco tortillas work great and are naturally pliable. Warm them for 15 seconds before filling.
How spicy are these tacos?
They have a medium, zesty buffalo kick. To dial down the heat, increase the melted butter in the sauce to 2 tablespoons.
