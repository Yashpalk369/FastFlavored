15-Minute Spicy Sichuan Dan Dan Noodles with Crispy Pork

Authentic Chengdu street food at home: springy noodles tossed in toasted sesame paste, Sichuan chili oil, and soy sauce, crowned with dark crispy pork crumbles and crushed peanuts.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute spicy Sichuan Dan Dan noodles, heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over high heat. Add 1/2 lb ground pork; stir-fry for 5 minutes, breaking into fine crumbles until deeply browned and crispy. Add 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp soy sauce, and 1 tsp hoisin sauce; toss for 60 seconds and set aside. In a measuring pitcher, whisk 3 tbsp Chinese sesame paste (or tahini + peanut butter), 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp Sichuan chili oil with flakes, 1 tbsp Chinkiang black vinegar, 1 tsp ground Sichuan pepper, 1 tsp sugar, and 1.5 cups hot chicken broth. Boil 10 oz Chinese wheat noodles and 2 halved baby bok choy for 3 minutes; drain. Divide the warm sesame chili broth into 4 bowls, add noodles and bok choy, and crown with crispy pork crumbles, crushed peanuts, and green onions. Toss together with chopsticks and enjoy!

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Sesame Paste & Black Vinegar Synergy: Nutty toasted sesame paste combined with acidic, malty Chinkiang black vinegar forms the rich emulsified broth foundation that defines Chengdu street food.
  • Crispy Pork Caramelization: Frying ground pork until moisture evaporates leaves behind dark, crispy savory nuggets that provide crunchy contrast against soft noodles.
  • Tingle of Sichuan Pepper: Ground Sichuan peppercorns contribute signature floral citrus numbness (málà) that elevates the dish beyond ordinary chili heat.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Crisp the Ground Pork: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over high heat. Add ground pork and fry for 5 minutes, breaking it into fine crumbles with a spatula until all liquid evaporates and the meat turns deeply browned and crispy. Stir in minced garlic, 1 tbsp soy sauce, and 1 tsp hoisin; cook for 60 seconds until fragrant. Remove from heat.
  2. Whisk Dan Dan Sauce Broth: In a medium bowl or measuring cup, whisk sesame paste, 3 tbsp soy sauce, chili oil, black vinegar, ground Sichuan pepper, sugar, and hot chicken broth until silky and emulsified.
  3. Boil Noodles & Bok Choy: Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil. Add noodles and halved bok choy; boil for 2.5 to 3 minutes until noodles are chewy and bok choy is bright green. Drain well in a colander.
  4. Assemble the Bowls: Divide the warm, nutty sesame chili broth among 4 serving bowls. Drop the drained noodles into the center of each bowl and arrange bok choy along the side.
  5. Top & Mix: Spoon generous portions of the crispy browned pork crumbles over the noodles. Garnish with chopped peanuts and sliced scallions. Use chopsticks to vigorously mix the noodles into the fiery sesame broth before slurping!

Elena’s Chinkiang Black Vinegar Secret

If you don't own Chinkiang black vinegar (Zhenjiang vinegar), pick up a bottle at an Asian market! Made from fermented glutinous rice, it has a rich, smoky, malted umami acidity with none of the harsh sour bite of white vinegar. It transforms Dan Dan noodle broth from ordinary into award-winning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this vegetarian / vegan?
Yes! Substitute finely diced shiitake mushrooms or plant-based ground meat for the pork, and use vegetable broth.
How do I control the Sichuan peppercorn numbness?
Sichuan pepper produces a tingling 'mala' sensation on the tongue. If you're new to it, start with 1/2 teaspoon and increase to taste!
Can I use spaghetti noodles?
In a pinch, standard spaghetti cooked al dente in water with 1 tsp baking soda mimics the bouncy chew of alkaline Chinese wheat noodles!
