Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute fluffy lemon ricotta pancakes, whisk together 1 cup whole-milk ricotta, 2 egg yolks, 1/2 cup milk, melted butter, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Gently combine with 1.25 cups flour, sugar, baking powder, and baking soda. Whip the 2 egg whites for 1 minute until soft peaks form, then fold into the batter. Cook 1/3-cup scoops on a buttered griddle over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and puffed.

Why Ricotta Elevates Ordinary Pancakes

  • Custardy, Soufflé Texture: Ricotta curds create tiny pockets of moisture that keep the pancakes velvety soft even as they cool.
  • Bright Citrus Balance: The sweet creaminess of Italian ricotta cuts through the zesty lemon oils for irresistible bakery aroma.
  • Double Leavening Power: Fresh lemon juice activates the baking soda, causing the batter to expand into tall, cloud-like rounds.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Dry Mix: Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Wet Mix: In another bowl, whisk ricotta, egg yolks, milk, melted butter, lemon zest, lemon juice, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Fold Whipped Whites: Whip egg whites 1 minute until soft peaks form. Fold ricotta mixture into dry ingredients, then gently fold in whipped whites.
  4. Griddle: Cook 1/3-cup scoops in a buttered pan over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes until bubbles appear. Flip and cook 1–2 minutes until puffed and golden.
  5. Serve Warm: Top with fresh wild blueberries, lemon curd, and warm maple syrup!

Elena’s Whipped White Lift

The 60-Second Egg White Fold: Separating your eggs and whipping the whites for just 60 seconds with a handheld fork traps microscopic air bubbles inside the batter. When folded into the ricotta base, those air bubbles expand on the griddle, transforming ordinary pancakes into towering, cloud-like Japanese soufflé pancakes!

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does ricotta make pancakes so fluffy?
Whole-milk ricotta adds delicate moisture and structure without making the batter heavy like excess milk or oil would. It produces a souffle-like, custardy interior with a melt-in-your-mouth crumb.
Do I really need to whip the egg whites?
Whipping the egg whites separately takes just 90 seconds and gives these pancakes their signature sky-high restaurant fluffiness. However, if you are in a rush, you can whisk whole eggs directly into the batter—they will still taste delicious and custardy!
Can I freeze cooked lemon ricotta pancakes?
Yes! Allow pancakes to cool completely, stack them with parchment paper squares in between, and freeze in a zip-top bag for up to 2 months. Reheat in a toaster or toaster oven straight from frozen for 2 minutes.
