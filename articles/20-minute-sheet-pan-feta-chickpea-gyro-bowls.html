20-Minute Sheet-Pan Crispy Roasted Feta and Chickpea Gyro Bowls

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan roasted feta and chickpea gyro bowls, drain and thoroughly pat dry two 15-oz cans of chickpeas. Cut an 8-oz block of Greek feta cheese into 1-inch cubes. On a large rimmed baking sheet, toss chickpeas and feta cubes with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp cumin, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Spread into a single layer and roast at 425°F (220°C) for 14 minutes, switching to high broil for the final 2 minutes until chickpeas are crunchy and feta edges are deeply caramelized. Assemble into bowls with warm turmeric yellow rice, sliced Persian cucumbers, halved cherry tomatoes, kalamata olives, pickled red onions, and a generous swirl of chilled tzatziki.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Drying Chickpeas for Crunch: Rolling canned chickpeas between kitchen towels to remove moisture and loose skins ensures they roast into crunchy golden bites instead of remaining soft.
  • Caramelized Block Feta: Roasting whole milk Greek feta at 425°F browns the exterior with savory golden edges while leaving the center warm, creamy, and spreadable.
  • Complete Plant-Based Protein: Combining chickpeas with yellow rice and feta cheese delivers a satisfying 19 grams of protein and 8 grams of prebiotic fiber per bowl.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat & Dry Chickpeas: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Pour drained chickpeas onto a clean kitchen towel or paper towels. Gently roll them dry, discarding any loose skins that slip off.
  2. Season on Sheet Pan: Transfer dried chickpeas and cubed feta cheese to a large rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle with 2 tbsp olive oil. Sprinkle with dried oregano, garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin, and cracked black pepper. Gently toss with hands to coat, taking care not to crush the feta cubes.
  3. Roast & Broil: Spread into a single layer across the sheet pan. Roast at 425°F for 14 minutes. Switch oven to HIGH BROIL for the last 2 minutes until chickpea skins are crackled and feta edges are blistered and deeply golden.
  4. Assemble the Bowls: Divide warm yellow rice into 4 wide bowls. Spoon the sizzling crispy chickpeas and warm roasted feta cubes over one side.
  5. Add Fresh Toppings & Tzatziki: Arrange sliced cucumbers, halved tomatoes, kalamata olives, and pickled red onions around the bowl. Top each with a generous dollop of creamy tzatziki, fresh dill fronds, and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice!

Elena’s Block Feta Rule

Always buy block feta preserved in brine rather than pre-crumbled tubs! Pre-crumbled feta is coated in anti-caking starches that dry out and burn into rubbery bits in the oven. Cutting a block of feta yourself ensures luscious, creamy centers with golden crispy roasted edges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this in an air fryer?
Yes! Air fry the chickpeas and feta cubes at 390°F for 8–10 minutes, shaking gently halfway through until golden and crispy.
Can I substitute cauliflower rice?
Yes! For a low-carb grain bowl, swap the yellow rice for roasted garlic cauliflower rice.
How do I store leftovers?
Store the roasted chickpeas and feta in an airtight container for up to 4 days. Reheat in the toaster oven or skillet for 3 minutes to crisp back up before topping fresh veggies.
