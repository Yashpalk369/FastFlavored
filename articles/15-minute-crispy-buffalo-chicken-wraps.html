Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy buffalo chicken wraps, air fry or sear 1 lb of crispy chicken cutlets until crunchy (6–8 minutes), then toss in 1/3 cup hot sauce whisked with 2 tbsp melted butter. Warm 4 large flour tortillas, layer with shredded lettuce and cheddar cheese, top with the warm buffalo chicken and diced tomatoes, and drizzle with cool buttermilk ranch dressing. Roll tightly and toast seam-side down in a hot skillet for 1 minute before slicing.

Why Everyone Obsesses Over These Wraps

  • Restaurant Quality at Home: The combination of spicy buffalo glaze, cool creamy ranch, and crunchy chicken replicates high-end pub grub in 15 minutes.
  • Zero Stress Cleanup: Built directly on your cutting board with minimal utensils or pans to wash.
  • Customizable Heat: Whisking real butter into cayenne pepper sauce cuts the harsh vinegar bite, creating silky, balanced buffalo richness.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Crisp Chicken: Warm or air-fry crispy chicken cutlets at 400°F (204°C) for 6–8 minutes until hot and crunch-ready. Slice into strips.
  2. Toss in Sauce: Whisk melted butter and hot sauce together in a bowl. Toss chicken strips until coated in glistening buffalo glaze.
  3. Warm Wraps: Warm tortillas for 15 seconds in a dry pan so they bend without tearing.
  4. Layer & Drizzle: Place lettuce and cheddar cheese in the center of each wrap, followed by warm buffalo chicken and tomatoes. Drizzle generously with ranch dressing.
  5. Roll & Toast: Fold sides inward and roll tightly. Toast seam-side down in a hot skillet for 1 minute for a golden seal. Cut diagonally and serve!

Elena’s Anti-Soggy Lettuce Barrier

The Lettuce-First Architecture: Never pour ranch or place hot buffalo sauce directly onto raw tortillas! It dissolves the wheat starches within minutes. Always create a protective moisture barrier by layering cold shredded lettuce and cheese against the tortilla first, cushioning the hot saucy chicken in the middle!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you keep buffalo chicken wraps from getting soggy?
Create a barrier! Lay the shredded lettuce and cheese directly against the tortilla first, placing the saucy chicken in the center. Lightly toasting the rolled wrap seam-side down on a dry skillet also crisps the exterior and seals in moisture.
Can I meal prep these wraps ahead of time?
Yes! Store the tossed chicken, veggies, and dressing separately in containers. Assemble the wraps right before packing or eating so the tortilla stays fresh and soft.
Blue cheese or ranch dressing?
Buttermilk ranch is the crowd favorite for family dinners because of its cool herb creaminess, but authentic buffalo wing purists love chunky blue cheese dressing. Both work deliciously!
