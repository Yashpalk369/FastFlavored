15-Minute Sweet Thai Chili Garlic Shrimp Rice Bowls

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute sweet chili shrimp rice bowls, pat 1.25 lbs raw peeled and deveined jumbo shrimp completely dry and season with 1/2 tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large skillet over high heat; sear shrimp in a single layer for 90 seconds per side until pink and caramelized. Reduce heat to medium, add 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1/3 cup Thai sweet chili sauce, 1.5 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, and 1 tsp sriracha (for a fiery kick). Toss shrimp continuously for 60 seconds as the glaze bubbles and turns thick and sticky. Spoon immediately over bowls of warm jasmine rice with sliced cucumbers, matchstick carrots, green scallions, and toasted sesame seeds.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Blotting Shrimp Dry: Removing surface water ensures shrimp sear vigorously in hot oil instead of boiling in released liquids, developing sweet caramelized edges in 3 minutes.
  • Sweet Chili Soy Glaze Reduction: Combining sweet chili sauce with savory soy sauce balances high sweetness with salty umami, caramelizing onto the shrimp without cloying heaviness.
  • Crisp Raw Vegetable Texture: Crunchy Persian cucumbers and raw matchstick carrots provide a refreshing thermal and textural contrast to hot glazed seafood.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season the Shrimp: Thoroughly pat peeled shrimp dry with paper towels. Season with garlic powder, salt, and freshly ground black pepper.
  2. Flash-Sear the Shrimp: Heat oil in a large cast iron skillet or wok over high heat until smoking hot. Add shrimp in a single layer with space between them. Sear undisturbed for 90 seconds until the underside is pink and caramelized. Flip and sear for another 60 seconds.
  3. Glaze in Sweet Chili Sauce: Reduce heat to medium. Add minced garlic and grated ginger to the pan drippings, stirring for 30 seconds. Pour in sweet chili sauce, soy sauce, sriracha, and lime juice. Toss the shrimp continuously as the glaze bubbles violently and turns into a thick, glossy lacquer (about 60 seconds).
  4. Assemble the Bowls: Divide warm steamed jasmine rice into 4 shallow bowls. Pile the sweet chili glazed shrimp and extra pan sauce over the rice.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Tuck cucumber slices and shredded carrots alongside the shrimp. Shower with green scallions, toasted sesame seeds, and extra lime wedges. Serve piping hot!

Elena’s 3-Minute Shrimp Timer Rule

Jumbo shrimp are completely cooked when they form a loose 'C' shape! If they curl into a tight, closed 'O' shape, they have been overcooked and will turn rubbery. Pull them off the heat the exact second they curl into a gentle 'C' and let the resting glaze finish the magic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this with frozen shrimp?
Yes! Thaw frozen shrimp quickly in a bowl of cold water for 15 minutes, peel, and pat thoroughly dry with paper towels before seasoning.
Can I use cauliflower rice for low-carb?
Yes, garlic sautéed cauliflower rice is delicious with sweet chili shrimp and cuts the carbs significantly.
How spicy is Thai sweet chili sauce?
It is very mild and sweet with faint chili warmth. Adding the 1 tsp sriracha balances the sweetness into a gentle medium kick.
