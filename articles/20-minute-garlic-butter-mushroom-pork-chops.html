20-Minute Creamy Garlic Butter Mushroom Pork Chops Skillet

Thick golden pan-seared pork chops smothered in a rich garlic butter and caramelized cremini mushroom cream sauce in a cast-iron skillet in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute garlic butter mushroom pork chops, season 4 boneless center-cut pork chops (3/4-inch thick) with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Sear in 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for 4 minutes per side until golden brown (145°F internal); remove to a plate. In the same skillet, melt 2 tbsp butter and sauté 8 oz sliced cremini mushrooms and 4 minced garlic cloves for 4 minutes until deeply caramelized. Pour in 1/2 cup chicken broth and 1/2 cup heavy cream with 1 tsp fresh thyme; simmer 3 minutes until thick. Return pork chops to coat and serve.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • 145°F Pork Juiciness Rule: Pulling pork chops at 145°F with a 3-minute rest ensures succulent, juicy meat with a tender rosy center rather than dry gray chops.
  • Caramelized Cremini Mushroom Umami: Browned cremini mushrooms release deep glutamate umami that transforms simple heavy cream and pan drippings into gourmet steakhouse sauce.
  • Cast-Iron Golden Crust: High thermal mass creates a sizzling mahogany sear on pork chops in just 4 minutes per side.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season & Sear Pork Chops: Pat pork chops completely dry with paper towels; season both sides with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add pork chops and sear undisturbed for 4 minutes until deeply golden. Flip and sear 3 to 4 minutes more until internal temperature reaches 145°F (63°C). Transfer to a plate and tent loosely with foil.
  2. Caramelize Cremini Mushrooms: Melt remaining 2 tbsp butter in the same skillet over medium-high heat. Add sliced cremini mushrooms and spread across the pan. Sauté undisturbed for 2 minutes to brown, then stir and cook 2 minutes more until deeply caramelized and tender.
  3. Add Garlic Aromatics: Stir minced garlic and fresh thyme leaves into the browned mushrooms. Sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant, scraping up any delicious pork fond from the skillet bottom.
  4. Simmer Velvet Garlic Cream Sauce: Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream. Bring to a steady simmer and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the sauce reduces, turns golden-brown from the mushrooms, and coats the back of a spoon.
  5. Return Pork Chops & Serve: Nestle pork chops and accumulated resting juices back into the skillet. Spoon the rich mushroom cream sauce generously over the chops for 60 seconds until heated through. Garnish with fresh thyme sprigs and parsley. Serve immediately with mashed potatoes or steamed greens!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Do not salt your mushrooms until they have already browned in the hot butter! Salting raw mushrooms immediately draws out their cellular moisture, causing them to boil in their own liquid rather than caramelizing. Sauté them dry in butter first for maximum deep mahogany flavor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this with chicken instead?
Yes! Boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs work wonderfully and cook in the exact same mushroom cream sauce.
Can I use white button mushrooms?
Yes, white button mushrooms work fine, though cremini (baby bella) mushrooms have richer, deeper earthy umami that complements the pork.
What are the best side dishes for creamy pork chops?
Creamy Yukon gold mashed potatoes, buttered egg noodles, roasted asparagus, or crusty sourdough bread for mopping up every drop of mushroom cream.
