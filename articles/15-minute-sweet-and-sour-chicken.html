15-Minute Crispy Sweet and Sour Chicken Bites

Crispy bite-sized golden chicken breast pieces tossed in a tangy ruby-red sweet and sour sauce with fresh bell peppers, juicy pineapple, and toasted sesame seeds in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy sweet and sour chicken, toss 1.25 lbs bite-sized chicken breast chunks in 3 tbsp cornstarch, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp white pepper. Pan-fry in 2 tbsp vegetable oil over medium-high heat for 6–7 minutes until golden and crunchy. In a small bowl, whisk 1/3 cup pineapple juice, 3 tbsp ketchup, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tbsp brown sugar, and 1 tsp cornstarch. Pour into the skillet with 1 cup bell pepper cubes and 1 cup pineapple chunks; toss for 2 minutes until glossy and bubbling. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds and sliced green onions.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Cornstarch Crust Lock: Tossing chicken bites in pure cornstarch instead of flour forms an ultra-thin, crackly shell that stays shatteringly crisp even after coating in hot sauce.
  • Balanced Vinegar-Pineapple Glaze: Using real pineapple juice alongside rice vinegar and brown sugar delivers authentic bright acidity without relying on artificial red food dye.
  • High-Heat 2-Minute Reduction: Adding cold cornstarch slurry directly within the sauce thickens it into a glossy restaurant-quality coat in just 120 seconds of bubbling.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep & Coat Chicken: Pat chicken cubes completely dry with paper towels. In a medium bowl, toss chicken with 2.5 tbsp cornstarch, salt, and white pepper until every piece is dusted in a powdery dry coating.
  2. Pan-Sear Chicken to Golden Crisp: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large non-stick skillet or wok over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add chicken pieces in an even layer (do not overcrowd). Cook undisturbed for 3–4 minutes until deeply golden on the bottom, then flip and cook 3 minutes more until cooked through (165°F). Transfer to a plate.
  3. Sauté Aromatics & Peppers: In the same hot pan, toss in diced bell peppers, red onion petals, and pineapple chunks. Stir-fry for 2 minutes until blistered and tender-crisp.
  4. Whisk & Bubble Glossy Sauce: In a small measuring cup, whisk pineapple juice, ketchup, rice vinegar, brown sugar, soy sauce, minced garlic, and the remaining 1/2 tbsp cornstarch. Pour into skillet with the peppers; bring to a rapid boil for 60 seconds until glossy and thick.
  5. Toss, Garnish & Serve: Return crispy chicken to skillet and toss vigorously for 30–45 seconds until each bite is glistening in hot sweet and sour sauce. Scatter toasted sesame seeds and sliced scallions on top. Serve immediately over steamed jasmine rice!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Always toss the chicken in the sweet and sour sauce right before serving—never let it simmer in the bubbling liquid! Tossing off the heat seals the crispy crust in a high-gloss lacquer while keeping the interior succulent and tender.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use chicken thighs instead of breasts?
Yes! Boneless skinless chicken thighs work brilliantly and provide even juicier flavor. Cook them 1 minute longer for thorough rendering.
Can I make this gluten-free?
Absolutely! Substitute regular soy sauce with gluten-free tamari or coconut aminos. Cornstarch is naturally 100% gluten-free.
How do I store and reheat leftovers?
Store leftover sweet and sour chicken in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in an air fryer or dry skillet at 375°F for 4–5 minutes to restore the crispy crust.
