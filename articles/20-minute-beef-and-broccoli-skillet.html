Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute beef and broccoli that rivals top Chinese restaurants, thinly slice flank steak against the grain and toss with 1 tablespoon of cornstarch (velveting). Steam broccoli in 3 tablespoons of water directly in the skillet for 2 minutes, then flash-sear the beef over high heat for 90 seconds. Finish by simmering the garlic-ginger soy glaze for 60 seconds until rich, glossy, and clinging to every bite.

Why This Skillet Beats Takeout Every Time

  • Flash Cooking: High-heat searing preserves broccoli crunch and prevents the beef from getting tough or steamed.
  • Velveting Magic: A 5-minute cornstarch coat locks in interior juices while providing a delicate crust.
  • Controlled Sodium & Sugars: Made with pure low-sodium soy sauce, real garlic, and fresh grated ginger without excess oil.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Velvet Beef: In a bowl, toss sliced beef with 1 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp cornstarch. Let sit 5 minutes.
  2. Mix Sauce: Whisk remaining soy sauce, beef broth, brown sugar, minced garlic, ginger, and remaining 1 tbsp cornstarch.
  3. Steam Broccoli: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over high heat. Add broccoli and 3 tbsp water; cover with lid and steam for 2 to 3 minutes. Remove to a plate.
  4. Sear Beef: Add 1 tbsp oil to the hot skillet. Sear beef ribbons for 90 seconds without stirring, flip, and cook 1 more minute until browned.
  5. Glaze & Serve: Pour in the sauce. Simmer 1 minute until glossy and bubbling. Return broccoli, toss together, and top with sesame seeds and scallions.

Elena’s Velveting Secret

10-Minute Freezer Slicing Trick: Flank steak is easiest to slice wafer-thin when it is partially firm. Pop your steak into the freezer for 10 to 15 minutes before cooking. A sharp chef's knife will glide right through across the grain effortlessly!

Frequently Asked Questions

What cut of beef is best for quick beef and broccoli?
Flank steak is the gold standard because it slices easily across the grain into wide, tender ribbons. Top sirloin or skirt steak are also fantastic, juicy alternatives for flash stir-frying.
How do you make beef taste tender like Chinese restaurant takeout?
The secret is quick 'velveting': coating the thinly sliced beef in a tablespoon of cornstarch and soy sauce before searing. The cornstarch seals in natural juices and creates a velvety, melt-in-your-mouth outer coating.
