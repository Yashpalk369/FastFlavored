20-Minute Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Chicken and Tortellini Skillet

Juicy sliced golden chicken cutlets and tender cheese tortellini simmered in a decadent garlic sun-dried tomato and baby spinach parmesan cream sauce in 20 minutes.

By Elena Bennett • 20 min cook • 540 kcal • 44g Protein

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy chicken tortellini, season 1 lb thin chicken cutlets with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper; sear in 1 tbsp olive oil in a deep skillet for 4 minutes per side until golden (165°F), then slice into strips. In the same skillet, sauté 4 minced garlic cloves and 1/3 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes in 1 tbsp butter for 1 minute. Pour in 1 cup chicken broth and 3/4 cup heavy cream; bring to a boil. Add 9 oz refrigerated cheese tortellini and simmer uncovered for 4 minutes until tender. Stir in 2 cups baby spinach and 1/2 cup grated parmesan until wilted. Return sliced chicken to skillet and serve.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Refrigerated Tortellini Direct-Boil: Cooking fresh tortellini directly in simmering cream and chicken broth infuses pasta dough with Tuscan aromatics in 4 minutes.
  • Sun-Dried Tomato Acid Sparkle: Sweet and tart sun-dried tomatoes cut through the heavy cream and parmesan for a well-rounded restaurant palate.
  • Single-Skillet Complete Protein: Combining lean chicken breast cutlets with three-cheese tortellini delivers over 44g of satisfying protein per plate.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sear Golden Chicken Cutlets: Pat chicken cutlets dry; season both sides with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken cutlets and sear for 3 to 4 minutes per side until deeply golden and cooked through (165°F). Transfer to a cutting board and slice into strips.
  2. Sauté Garlic & Sun-Dried Tomatoes: Melt 1 tbsp butter in the same skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sliced sun-dried tomatoes; sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Build Tuscan Cream Broth: Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream, scraping up the browned chicken bits from the bottom with a wooden spoon. Bring to a rapid rolling simmer.
  4. Simmer Tortellini Directly in Sauce: Add refrigerated cheese tortellini straight into the bubbling liquid. Cook uncovered for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tortellini are plump, tender, and the sauce is reduced and creamy.
  5. Wilt Spinach & Return Chicken: Reduce heat to low. Stir in fresh baby spinach and grated parmesan cheese until spinach wilts and cheese melts into a velvety sauce. Return sliced chicken cutlets and any resting juices back to the skillet. Garnish with torn fresh basil and shaved parmesan. Serve immediately!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Simmer the tortellini right in the broth and heavy cream! As the fresh pasta cooks, it releases microscopic starches that naturally thicken the heavy cream into a restaurant-grade velvet sauce without needing a drop of flour or cornstarch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen tortellini?
Yes! Frozen tortellini will take about 6 to 7 minutes to cook through in the simmering sauce. Add an extra splash (2 tbsp) of chicken broth to account for cooking time.
Can I make this lighter?
You can substitute half-and-half for heavy cream, but simmer gently on medium-low to prevent curdling. Heavy cream provides the most velvety consistency.
How do I store and reheat leftovers?
Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop with 2 tablespoons of chicken broth or milk to loosen the sauce.
