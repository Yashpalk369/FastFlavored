Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute creamy garlic butter Tuscan gnocchi, drop 1 lb potato gnocchi into boiling salted water for 2 to 3 minutes until they float, then drain. Meanwhile, melt 2 tbsp butter with 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Sauté 4 minced garlic cloves and 1/2 cup sliced oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes for 60 seconds. Pour in 1/2 cup vegetable broth, 3/4 cup heavy cream, and 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese, simmering for 2 minutes until glossy and bubbling. Toss in the drained gnocchi and 3 cups fresh baby spinach, cooking for 60 seconds until spinach is wilted and gnocchi is coated in velvety cream. Garnish with fresh basil and cracked pepper.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • 3-Minute Gnocchi Float Rule: Potato gnocchi cook faster than dried pasta; pull them from the boiling water the exact second they bob to the surface to retain light, tender fluffiness.
  • Sun-Dried Tomato Sweetness & Acid: Using tomatoes packed in olive oil introduces natural herbs and tang that cuts through the rich heavy cream without curdling.
  • Parmesan Thickening Emulsion: Freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano melts into the simmering cream within 90 seconds, creating a restaurant-grade velvet sauce without flour or cornstarch.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil Gnocchi: Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add potato gnocchi and boil for 2 to 3 minutes until they all float to the surface. Drain in a colander and set aside.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: While gnocchi boils, melt butter and olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic, sliced sun-dried tomatoes, and crushed red pepper flakes. Sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant without browning.
  3. Build Velvet Cream Sauce: Pour in vegetable broth, heavy cream, and grated parmesan cheese. Whisk continuously for 2 minutes as the sauce comes to a gentle simmer and emulsifies into a glossy, velvety consistency.
  4. Toss Gnocchi & Wilt Spinach: Add drained gnocchi and fresh baby spinach directly into the skillet. Toss gently with a wooden spoon for 60 to 90 seconds until the spinach is wilted and every gnocchi is coated in rich garlic cream.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Remove from heat immediately. Scatter torn fresh basil leaves, extra grated parmesan, and freshly cracked black pepper over the top. Serve hot straight from the skillet!

Elena’s One-Pan Skillet Sear Alternative

If you don't feel like boiling a separate pot of water, you can pan-sear the gnocchi straight out of the package in 2 tbsp olive oil over medium-high heat for 6 minutes until crispy and golden on the outside! Then build the sauce right in the same pan for crispy-outside, pillowy-inside gnocchi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add protein to this dish?
Yes! Seared jumbo shrimp, sliced grilled chicken breast, or browned Italian sausage crumbles pair exquisitely.
Can I make this dairy-free?
Substitute full-fat canned coconut milk for heavy cream and use nutritional yeast or vegan parmesan.
How do I reheat leftover gnocchi?
Reheat in a skillet over medium-low heat with a splash of broth or cream to loosen the sauce; avoid the microwave which can make gnocchi gummy.
