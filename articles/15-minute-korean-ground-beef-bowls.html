Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute Korean ground beef bowls, brown 1 pound of lean ground beef with sliced scallion whites in a hot skillet for 5 minutes until crispy in spots. Whisk together 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce, 3 tablespoons brown sugar, 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil, minced garlic, and grated ginger. Pour over the beef and simmer for 2 minutes until glossy and caramelized, then serve over warm rice with sesame seeds.

Why This 15-Minute Bowl Is a Weeknight Lifesaver

  • Bulgogi Flavor Without the Marinating: Captures authentic sweet-savory Korean BBQ flavors without hours of slicing or marinating beef.
  • Pantry-Only Staples: Uses simple ground beef, soy sauce, sesame oil, and garlic—items you already have in the kitchen.
  • Ideal for Meal Prep: Holds up extraordinarily well in glass lunch containers for up to 4 days without losing moisture.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix Glaze: In a small bowl, whisk soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and red pepper flakes until blended.
  2. Brown Beef: Heat a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and scallion whites. Brown for 5 to 6 minutes, breaking into crumbles. Drain excess grease if desired.
  3. Glaze & Caramelize: Pour the sauce into the skillet. Simmer on medium-low for 2 to 3 minutes until thick, syrupy, and clinging to the beef.
  4. Assemble Bowls: Divide warm rice into bowls, spoon glazed beef over top, and garnish generously with sliced scallion greens and toasted sesame seeds.

Elena’s Caramelization Tip

Let It Sizzle Undisturbed: Don't stir the ground beef constantly! Allow it to sit flat on the hot skillet for 2 minutes before breaking it up. This creates golden, deeply caramelized crispy edges that provide wonderful texture contrast against the soft rice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I substitute ground turkey or chicken in this recipe?
Absolutely! Lean ground turkey or ground chicken works wonderfully. Because poultry has less natural fat, add 1 tablespoon of cooking oil to the skillet before browning so the meat remains tender.
How do I store and reheat leftovers for meal prep?
Store the cooked beef in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave with a tablespoon of water or in a hot skillet for 2 minutes to restore the glossy sauce.
