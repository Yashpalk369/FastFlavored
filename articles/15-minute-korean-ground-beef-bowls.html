15-Minute Sweet and Spicy Korean Ground Beef Bulgogi Bowls

Caramelized sweet and savory Korean ground beef with crispy edges, served over fluffy rice with quick pickled cucumbers, scallions, and sriracha drizzle in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute Korean ground beef bulgogi bowls, brown 1 lb lean ground beef (90/10) with 4 minced garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger in a wide skillet over high heat for 6 minutes, breaking into crumbles until crispy. In a small bowl, whisk 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce, 2.5 tbsp brown sugar, 1.5 tbsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tbsp gochujang (or sriracha), and 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper. Pour sauce over browned beef and bubble for 2 minutes until glossy and caramelized. Serve over steamed jasmine rice with quick-pickled cucumber slices and toasted sesame seeds.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Ground Beef Bulgogi Hack: Using ground beef instead of thinly sliced ribeye delivers the sweet, savory, and garlicky bulgogi flavor in 10 minutes flat at a fraction of the cost.
  • High-Heat Caramelization: Allowing the ground beef to sizzle undisturbed for 2 minutes before stirring creates irresistible dark caramelized crispy edges.
  • Quick Cucumber Acid Balance: Tossing thinly sliced Persian cucumbers in rice vinegar and sesame oil creates an instant cooling pickle that cuts beef richness.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Brown Beef to Crispy Edges: Heat a large skillet over high heat. Add ground beef and break apart with a wooden spatula. Cook undisturbed for 2–3 minutes to allow deep brown crusting, then flip, stir in minced garlic and grated ginger, and sauté for 3 more minutes until fully browned. Drain any excess fat if necessary.
  2. Whisk Sweet Savory Bulgogi Sauce: In a measuring cup, whisk low-sodium soy sauce, brown sugar, toasted sesame oil, gochujang, and red pepper flakes until sugar is dissolved.
  3. Simmer & Glaze Beef: Pour the sauce directly into the skillet with the hot beef. Cook over medium-high heat for 2 minutes, stirring continuously, as the sugars caramelize and reduce into a thick, glossy lacquer coating every crumb of beef.
  4. Quick-Pickle Cucumbers: While sauce bubbles, toss thinly sliced cucumbers with 1 tbsp rice vinegar and a pinch of salt in a small bowl.
  5. Assemble Bowls & Garnish: Divide warm jasmine rice into shallow bowls. Pile generous scoops of caramelized Korean beef on one side and pickled cucumber ribbons on the other. Drizzle with sriracha mayo, scatter toasted sesame seeds and sliced scallions, and serve immediately!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Let the ground beef sit flat against the hot skillet without touching it for the first 2 minutes! This allows the meat sugars and proteins to undergo the Maillard reaction, yielding crunchy caramelized bits that emulate authentic charcoal-grilled bulgogi.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I substitute for gochujang?
If you don't have Korean gochujang paste, use 1 tbsp sriracha or 1 tsp chili garlic sauce mixed with 1/2 tsp extra brown sugar.
Can I meal prep these bowls?
Yes! Korean ground beef reheats exceptionally well and stays moist for up to 4 days in the fridge. Pack cucumbers separately so they stay crisp.
Can I make this lower carb?
Serve over cauliflower rice or wrap the hot Korean beef in crisp butter lettuce leaves or bibb lettuce cups for a delicious keto meal.
