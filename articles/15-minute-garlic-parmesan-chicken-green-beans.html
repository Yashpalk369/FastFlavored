15-Minute Crispy Garlic Butter Parmesan Green Beans and Chicken

Golden seared chicken tenderloin strips and blistered tender-crisp green beans glistening in a rich garlic butter sauce showered with parmesan and lemon in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute garlic parmesan chicken and green beans, season 1.25 lbs chicken tenderloins with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Sear in 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes per side until golden (165°F); transfer to a plate. In the same hot pan, melt 2 tbsp butter and add 12 oz trimmed fresh green beans with 4 minced garlic cloves and 2 tbsp water; cover for 3 minutes to steam-sear until blistered and tender-crisp. Return chicken to skillet, remove from heat, shower with 1/2 cup grated parmesan and lemon zest, and serve.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Steam-Sear Green Bean Technique: Adding 2 tablespoons of water and covering the hot skillet for 3 minutes steams the beans tender while blister-searing their bottoms in foaming butter.
  • Chicken Tenderloin Flash Cooking: Using tenderloins eliminates carving time and cooks through to juicy tenderness in under 7 total minutes.
  • Off-Heat Parmesan Melt: Showering grated parmesan off the flame creates a delicate nutty cheese crust that adheres to the beans without clumping.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season & Sear Chicken Tenderloins: Pat chicken tenderloins dry with paper towels; season both sides with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until hot. Add chicken and sear for 3 to 4 minutes per side until deeply golden and cooked through to 165°F. Transfer chicken to a clean plate.
  2. Steam-Sear Fresh Green Beans: Melt remaining 2 tbsp butter in the same skillet over medium-high heat. Add trimmed green beans, minced garlic, and 2 tablespoons of water. Immediately cover skillet with a lid or foil for 3 minutes to steam the green beans tender.
  3. Blister & Brown Beans: Remove the lid and toss green beans in the sizzling garlic butter over high heat for 1 to 2 minutes until excess liquid evaporates and the beans are blistered with golden char marks.
  4. Combine & Melt Parmesan: Return cooked chicken tenderloins and resting juices back into the skillet. Remove skillet from direct heat. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the chicken and beans, then shower the entire pan with grated parmesan cheese and lemon zest. Toss gently so the cheese melts over the warm vegetables and chicken.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Scatter fresh oregano or parsley and cracked black pepper over the skillet. Serve immediately right from the pan!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Cover the skillet with a lid for exactly 3 minutes when you add the green beans and water! The trapped steam rapidly penetrates the fibrous green beans to soften them to tender-crisp perfection in 180 seconds, while the sizzling butter at the bottom simultaneously browns their skins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen green beans?
Fresh green beans provide vastly superior texture. If using frozen green beans, thaw and dry thoroughly with paper towels before searing—skip the steam step.
Can I make this dairy-free?
Substitute butter with extra virgin olive oil or ghee, and replace parmesan cheese with nutritional yeast or dairy-free parmesan.
Can I substitute asparagus for green beans?
Yes! Fresh trimmed asparagus spears cook in the exact same 3-minute timeframe with zero adjustments.
