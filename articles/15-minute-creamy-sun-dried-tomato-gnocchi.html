15-Minute Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Gnocchi

Pillowy golden pan-seared potato gnocchi coated in an ultra-luscious garlic parmesan cream sauce with baby spinach, sweet sun-dried tomatoes, and melted mozzarella.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute creamy sun-dried tomato gnocchi without boiling, sear 1 lb of potato gnocchi straight from the package in butter and olive oil for 4–5 minutes until golden and crisp. Sauté minced garlic and sun-dried tomatoes, deglaze with vegetable broth and heavy cream, and simmer for 2 minutes. Stir in grated parmesan and baby spinach until wilted, fold in the crispy gnocchi, and top with fresh mozzarella pearls until melted.

Why Pan-Searing Gnocchi Changes Everything

  • Goodbye Mushy Gnocchi: Searing creates a crisp, golden crust on each dumpling while keeping the interior fluffy and pillowy.
  • Zero Boiling Required: No heavy pot of boiling water to wait for—one single skillet handles searing, sauce-making, and cheese melting.
  • Restaurant Quality in 15 Minutes: Rich heavy cream, sweet sun-dried tomatoes, and stretchy melted mozzarella create an authentic trattoria dinner in 15 minutes.

Cook Time 10 Mins
Total Time 15 Mins
Yield 4 Servings
Calories 460 kcal

Ingredients

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Pan-Sear Gnocchi: Melt 1 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add gnocchi straight from package; cook undisturbed 3–4 minutes until golden on bottom. Toss and cook 2 minutes more. Set aside on a plate.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: Melt remaining 1 tbsp butter over medium heat. Sauté garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, and chili flakes for 1 minute.
  3. Simmer Cream: Pour in broth and heavy cream, scraping up pan fond. Simmer 2–3 minutes. Reduce heat to low and whisk in parmesan until velvety.
  4. Wilt Spinach & Add Gnocchi: Add spinach; stir 1 minute until wilted. Return crisped gnocchi to the pan and toss gently to coat.
  5. Melt Mozzarella & Serve: Scatter mozzarella pearls on top, cover pan for 1 minute until gooey and melted. Top with fresh torn basil and cracked pepper. Serve hot!

Elena’s No-Boil Skillet Rule

Never Boil Packaged Gnocchi: Shelf-stable potato gnocchi is already pre-cooked in the package. Tossing it into boiling water hydrates the starches to excess, making the dumplings soggy. Instead, pan-frying directly in butter toasts the exterior into golden perfection while the steam inside fluffs the potato crumb!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need to boil gnocchi before searing it in the skillet?
No! Boiling gnocchi makes it waterlogged and gummy. Pan-searing dry gnocchi directly in foaming butter creates a golden, crisp exterior and a fluffy, pillow-soft interior without dirtying a pasta pot.
Can I add protein to this gnocchi skillet?
Yes! Sliced grilled chicken breast, sautéed garlic shrimp, or crumbled Italian sausage make delicious additions that pair seamlessly with the sun-dried tomato cream sauce.
Can I use cauliflower gnocchi?
Yes! If using frozen cauliflower gnocchi (like Trader Joe's), pan-fry in a hot skillet with 1 tablespoon of oil for 7–9 minutes until browned and crispy before tossing with the cream sauce.
