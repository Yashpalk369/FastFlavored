20-Minute Creamy French Dijon Tarragon Chicken Skillet

Tender golden pan-seared chicken cutlets simmered in a velvety French Dijon mustard, white wine, and fresh tarragon heavy cream sauce in just 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy Dijon chicken, season 4 thin chicken cutlets with salt and pepper, then sear in 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter for 4–5 minutes per side until golden (165°F); set aside. Deglaze the skillet with 1/4 cup dry white wine (or chicken broth) and 2 minced shallots for 2 minutes. Stir in 3/4 cup heavy cream, 2 tbsp smooth Dijon mustard, 1 tbsp whole grain mustard, and 1 tbsp chopped fresh tarragon. Simmer 3 minutes until thick, return chicken to coat, and spoon luscious sauce over top.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Dual-Mustard Depth: Blending smooth Dijon for sharp silkiness with stone-ground whole grain mustard provides textural pop and mellow, earthy acidity.
  • White Wine Pan Fond Deglazing: Deglazing caramelized chicken fond with dry Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc unlocks French bistro depth without long simmering.
  • Fresh Tarragon Lift: Fresh French tarragon with its subtle anise note balances the richness of the heavy cream and cuts through savory pan juices.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season & Sear Chicken Cutlets: Pat chicken cutlets dry with paper towels; season both sides generously with salt and cracked black pepper. Heat olive oil and butter in a large heavy skillet (stainless steel or cast-iron) over medium-high heat. Add chicken cutlets and sear undisturbed for 4 minutes per side until deeply golden and cooked through to 165°F. Transfer chicken to a warm plate.
  2. Sauté Shallots & Aromatics: Reduce skillet heat to medium. Add minced shallots and garlic into the remaining pan drippings; sauté for 90 seconds until fragrant, translucent, and tender.
  3. Deglaze the Skillet: Pour in white wine (or chicken broth), using a wooden spoon to scrape up all the delicious browned bits (fond) stuck to the bottom of the pan. Simmer vigorously for 2 minutes until liquid reduces by half.
  4. Simmer Dijon Cream Sauce: Stir in heavy cream, smooth Dijon mustard, whole grain mustard, and fresh chopped tarragon. Whisk gently until smoothly incorporated. Simmer for 3–4 minutes until sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon.
  5. Return Chicken & Serve: Nestle chicken cutlets and any accumulated resting juices back into the skillet. Spoon sauce over the cutlets and squeeze fresh lemon juice over the top. Garnish with fresh tarragon sprigs and serve immediately with crusty sourdough bread or roasted baby potatoes!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Always whisk the mustard into the heavy cream off direct high heat and let it gently simmer. Boiling Dijon at violent temperatures dulls the delicate mustard volatile oils and can cause dairy to break. Gentle heat guarantees a glossy, emulsified restaurant finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this without white wine?
Yes! Simply deglaze the pan with 1/4 cup low-sodium chicken broth mixed with 1 tsp fresh lemon juice or white wine vinegar.
Can I substitute dried tarragon?
Yes, but use only 1 tsp of dried tarragon as dried herbs are more concentrated. Fresh tarragon has a much brighter, fresher flavor if you can find it.
What are the best side dishes for this chicken?
It pairs exquisitely with buttered egg noodles, mashed Yukon gold potatoes, steamed French green beans (haricots verts), or warm crusty baguettes.
