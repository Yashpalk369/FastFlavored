15-Minute Creamy Tomato Spinach Tortellini Soup

Comforting restaurant-quality soup loaded with tender three-cheese tortellini and baby spinach simmering in a rich, creamy garlic tomato basil broth in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute creamy tomato tortellini soup, sauté 3 minced garlic cloves in 1 tbsp olive oil in a soup pot for 1 minute. Pour in 4 cups low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth, one 24-oz jar marinara (or crushed San Marzano tomatoes), 1 tsp Italian seasoning, and 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes; bring to a rolling boil. Add 9 oz refrigerated three-cheese tortellini and boil for 4–5 minutes until tender. Stir in 1/2 cup heavy cream, 3 cups fresh baby spinach, and 1/3 cup grated parmesan until wilted. Garnish with torn basil.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Refrigerated Tortellini Speed: Refrigerated cheese tortellini cook directly in the simmering tomato broth in just 4 minutes, infusing pasta dough with rich herbal flavor.
  • Marinara Broth Base: Using high-quality jarred marinara mixed with savory broth delivers slow-simmered all-day flavor in under 10 minutes flat.
  • Silk Heavy Cream Swirl: A half-cup splash of heavy cream tempers tomato acidity into a luxurious, velvety bisque consistency.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sauté Garlic Aromatics: Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy soup pot over medium heat. Add minced garlic and red pepper flakes; sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant without browning.
  2. Build Rich Tomato Broth: Pour in chicken or vegetable broth, marinara sauce, and Italian seasoning. Stir thoroughly to combine and bring to a rolling boil over high heat.
  3. Cook Fresh Tortellini: Add the refrigerated tortellini into the boiling tomato broth. Cook uncovered for 4 to 5 minutes at a steady simmer, stirring occasionally, until tortellini float to the top and are tender to the bite.
  4. Stir in Cream & Wilt Spinach: Reduce heat to low. Pour in heavy cream, fresh baby spinach, and grated parmesan cheese. Gently stir for 90 seconds until the spinach is tenderly wilted and the broth turns a luscious rosy pink color.
  5. Ladle & Garnish: Ladle steaming soup into wide shallow bowls. Top with freshly torn basil leaves, extra grated parmesan, and cracked black pepper. Serve alongside toasted sourdough or garlic bread!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: If you plan on having leftovers, cook the tortellini in a separate small pot of boiling water and add them to individual bowls! Leaving tortellini sitting in warm broth overnight causes them to absorb all liquid like a sponge and turn into pasta mush.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add meat to this soup?
Yes! Brown 1/2 lb of mild Italian sausage or ground turkey in the pot before adding the garlic, draining excess fat.
Can I substitute milk for heavy cream?
Whole milk or half-and-half can be used, but heavy cream yields the creamiest texture and prevents tomato acid from curdling the dairy.
Can I freeze this soup?
Freeze the soup base without tortellini and cream for up to 3 months. When reheating, bring to a boil, drop in tortellini, and finish with cream and spinach.
