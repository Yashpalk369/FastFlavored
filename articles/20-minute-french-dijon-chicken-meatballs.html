20-Minute Creamy French Dijon Tarragon Chicken Meatball Skillet

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy French Dijon tarragon chicken meatballs, mix 1.25 lbs lean ground chicken with 1/4 cup panko, 1 lightly beaten egg, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp chopped fresh tarragon, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper; gently roll into 12 meatballs. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Sear meatballs for 5 minutes until golden brown on all sides; transfer to a plate. Deglaze the skillet with 1/3 cup dry white wine (or chicken broth), scraping up browned fond. Whisk in 2 tbsp smooth Dijon mustard, 1 tbsp whole-grain mustard, 3/4 cup heavy cream, and 1/2 cup chicken broth. Return meatballs to the skillet, bring to a gentle simmer, and cook for 6 minutes until cooked through (165°F) and sauce thickens into velvet. Stir in 2 tbsp fresh tarragon and serve with egg noodles or buttered potatoes.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Two-Mustard French Balance: Combining smooth Dijon for sharp silkiness with whole-grain mustard for rustic pop gives the sauce genuine Parisian bistro complexity.
  • Fresh Tarragon Pairing: Fresh French tarragon has delicate sweet anise and herbal pepper notes that cut through the richness of heavy cream and poultry.
  • White Wine Pan Fond Deglaze: Deglazing the skillet with dry white wine lifts the caramelized chicken juices, infusing gentle acidity and restaurant depth.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix & Shape Chicken Meatballs: In a mixing bowl, combine ground chicken, panko, beaten egg, half the minced garlic, 1 tbsp chopped tarragon, 1/2 tsp salt, and pepper. Mix gently until just combined. Roll into 12 meatballs with damp hands.
  2. Sear Meatballs to Golden Brown: Heat olive oil and butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add meatballs and sear undisturbed for 2.5 minutes, turn and sear for another 2.5 minutes until browned on the exterior. Transfer meatballs to a plate.
  3. Deglaze Pan with White Wine: Pour dry white wine into the hot skillet, scraping up all browned savory bits from the bottom with a wooden spoon for 60 seconds until liquid reduces by half.
  4. Build Dijon Cream Sauce: Whisk in chicken broth, heavy cream, smooth Dijon mustard, whole-grain mustard, and remaining minced garlic. Bring to a gentle bubbling simmer.
  5. Simmer & Finish: Return the browned meatballs to the simmering sauce. Reduce heat to medium-low and cook uncovered for 6 to 7 minutes until meatballs reach 165°F and the sauce thickens into a glossy velvet glaze. Remove from heat, fold in remaining fresh tarragon, and serve over buttered egg noodles!

Elena’s Fresh Tarragon Mandate

If possible, always use fresh French tarragon instead of dried! Dried tarragon loses its delicate licorice-sweet aromatics and turns dusty. Fresh tarragon has glossy green leaves that release pure French bistro magic when folded into hot cream right at the end.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this without alcohol?
Yes! Simply replace the white wine with chicken broth plus 1 teaspoon of fresh lemon juice or white wine vinegar for acidity.
Can I use ground pork or veal?
Yes! A blend of pork and chicken or pork and veal is classic in French and Belgian cuisine and produces extraordinarily juicy meatballs.
How do I store leftovers?
Store meatballs and Dijon sauce in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days. Reheat gently in a skillet over low heat with a splash of broth.
