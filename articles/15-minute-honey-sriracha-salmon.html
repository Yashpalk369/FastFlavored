15-Minute Sweet and Spicy Honey Sriracha Glazed Salmon

Crispy pan-seared salmon fillets lacquered in an addictive sweet and fiery honey sriracha garlic glaze, finished with sesame seeds and lime in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute honey sriracha salmon, season 4 skin-on salmon fillets with salt and pepper. Sear skin-side down in 1 tbsp avocado oil in a stainless steel skillet over medium-high heat for 4 minutes until crispy; flip and sear 2 minutes more. In a small bowl, whisk 1/3 cup honey, 2 tbsp sriracha, 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tbsp lime juice, and 3 minced garlic cloves. Pour glaze into the skillet around the salmon, spooning continuously over the fillets for 2 minutes as it bubbles into a sticky, glossy lacquer. Garnish with scallions and sesame seeds.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Sticky Honey Lacquer Glaze: Simmering raw clover honey with fiery sriracha and umami soy sauce creates a restaurant-style glass glaze in 90 seconds.
  • High-Heat Skin Crisp: Searing salmon skin-down first renders healthy omega-3 fat into a crackly potato chip crust that contrasts tender flaky meat.
  • Lime Acidity Counterpunch: Fresh lime juice brightens the fiery garlic glaze and cuts through rich salmon oils for balanced restaurant flavor.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Dry & Season Salmon: Pat salmon fillets thoroughly dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt and cracked black pepper.
  2. Sear Skin-Side Down to Crispy: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide stainless steel or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place salmon fillets skin-side down and press gently with a spatula for 10 seconds. Sear undisturbed for 4 minutes until skin is deeply golden and crackly.
  3. Flip & Sear Flesh: Gently flip salmon fillets and cook for 2 minutes on the flesh side until nearly cooked through.
  4. Whisk & Bubble Glaze: In a small measuring cup, whisk honey, sriracha, soy sauce, minced garlic, and lime juice. Pour glaze directly into the skillet around the salmon. Cook over medium heat for 2 minutes, tilting pan and spooning the furiously bubbling glaze continuously over the salmon until it reduces to a sticky, mahogany lacquer coating every fillet.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Transfer glazed salmon onto plates or over bowls of fluffy jasmine rice. Spoon remaining sticky pan sauce over top. Sprinkle with sliced scallions, toasted sesame seeds, and serve with charred lime halves!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Pour the glaze in during the final 2 minutes of cooking! Honey contains natural sugars that can scorch and taste bitter if cooked for too long over high heat. Glazing at the very end creates a luscious glossy candy-like coating without a hint of burnt sugar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this less spicy?
Yes! Reduce sriracha to 1 tablespoon for a very mild sweet chili profile, or increase honey by 1 tablespoon.
Can I use skinless salmon fillets?
Yes! Skinless fillets work great; just sear for 3 minutes per side before pouring in the honey sriracha glaze.
What are the best vegetables to serve with this?
Steamed broccoli florets, blistered snap peas, garlic green beans, or baby bok choy all taste incredible dipped in the leftover pan glaze.
