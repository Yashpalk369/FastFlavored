15-Minute Crispy Bang Bang Shrimp Rice Bowls

Golden crispy fried jumbo shrimp tossed in a creamy sweet and spicy chili mayo bang bang sauce, served over jasmine rice with diced avocado in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy bang bang shrimp, peel and pat dry 1 lb raw jumbo shrimp. Toss with 3 tbsp cornstarch, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Pan-fry in 3 tbsp hot vegetable oil over medium-high heat for 2 minutes per side until golden and shatteringly crisp; drain on paper towels. In a medium bowl, whisk 1/3 cup mayonnaise, 3 tbsp sweet Thai chili sauce, 1 tbsp sriracha, and 1 tsp honey. Gently toss crispy hot shrimp in the sauce until coated, then serve immediately over warm jasmine rice with diced avocado and sliced green onions.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Cornstarch Crisp Shield: Tossing dry peeled shrimp in pure cornstarch creates a crackly, light tempura-style shell that holds onto creamy bang bang sauce without sogginess.
  • Sweet Chili Sriracha Mayo Emulsion: Whisking Kewpie or real mayonnaise with Thai sweet chili sauce and fiery sriracha delivers the world-famous Bonefish Grill bang bang flavor.
  • Instant 4-Minute Pan Fry: Shallow pan-frying in 3 tablespoons of hot oil crisps shrimp in 4 minutes flat with zero deep fryer mess.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Dry & Dredge Jumbo Shrimp: Pat peeled shrimp thoroughly dry with paper towels (dry shrimp is the key to crispiness). In a bowl, toss shrimp with cornstarch, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper until every piece is evenly coated in a dry white powdery film.
  2. Shallow Pan-Fry to Golden Crisp: Heat 3 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering hot (about 365°F). Add shrimp in a single layer without crowding. Fry for 2 minutes undisturbed until bottom is golden and crunchy, then flip and fry 90 seconds more until shrimp are pink and crisp. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain for 30 seconds.
  3. Whisk Famous Bang Bang Sauce: In a large mixing bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, sweet Thai chili sauce, sriracha, and honey until smooth and creamy. (Taste and adjust sriracha for your spice preference).
  4. Toss Shrimp in Creamy Glaze: Transfer the hot crispy shrimp into the bowl with the bang bang sauce. Gently toss with a silicone spatula for 30 seconds until every shrimp is lavishly coated in glossy pink sauce.
  5. Assemble Bowls & Garnish: Scoop warm jasmine rice into bowls, top with generous portions of sauced bang bang shrimp and diced creamy avocado. Garnish with sliced green scallions and toasted sesame seeds. Serve immediately while shrimp are shatteringly crisp!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Toss the shrimp in the bang bang sauce right before the bowls hit the table! Never let sauced shrimp sit on the counter, as the warm steam from the shrimp will gradually soften the crispy cornstarch coating. Tossing at the last second guarantees that legendary hot, crunchy, creamy contrast.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this in the air fryer?
Yes! Lightly spray the cornstarch-coated shrimp with cooking spray and air fry at 400°F (200°C) for 6 to 8 minutes, flipping halfway through. Toss in bang bang sauce immediately.
Can I make bang bang chicken instead?
Absolutely! Cut 1 lb boneless chicken breast into bite-sized nuggets, toss in cornstarch, and pan-fry for 6–7 minutes before tossing in sauce.
What can I substitute for mayonnaise?
You can substitute full-fat plain Greek yogurt for half or all of the mayonnaise for a lighter, higher-protein sauce with a pleasant tang.
