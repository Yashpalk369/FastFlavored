20-Minute Sheet-Pan Crispy Gnocchi with Italian Sausage and Peppers

Golden crispy roasted potato gnocchi, browned Italian sausage slices, charred tri-color bell peppers, and red onions tossed with basil pesto in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan crispy gnocchi with sausage and peppers, preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Slice 12 oz fully-cooked sweet Italian sausages into 1/2-inch coins. On a large rimmed baking sheet, toss one 16-oz package shelf-stable potato gnocchi (uncooked, right from package), sausage slices, 2 sliced bell peppers, and 1 sliced red onion with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Roast for 15 minutes, broiling the final 2 minutes until gnocchi are crackly golden and sausage is caramelized. Drizzle with 3 tbsp basil pesto and grated parmesan.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Unboiled Gnocchi Magic: Roasting dry shelf-stable potato gnocchi without boiling puffs them into pillowy, cloud-like centers surrounded by a shatteringly crisp crust.
  • Rendered Sausage Flavor Coating: Sausage fat renders across the sheet pan during roasting, infusing gnocchi and bell peppers with savory Italian fennel aromatics.
  • Post-Bake Pesto Finish: Drizzling basil pesto off the heat preserves vibrant green color and delicate basil oils without scorching in the hot oven.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat & Prep Sheet Pan: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large heavy rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper or foil for effortless cleanup.
  2. Combine & Season on Pan: Empty dry, uncooked potato gnocchi straight from package onto the baking sheet. Add sliced sausage coins, bell pepper strips, and red onion wedges. Drizzle everything with 2 tbsp olive oil and season with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Toss with hands until well distributed in an even single layer.
  3. Roast to Crispy Golden Glory: Roast at 425°F for 14 to 15 minutes, stirring once at the 10-minute mark to ensure even browning.
  4. Broil for Shattering Crunch: Turn oven to High Broil for the final 2 minutes, watching closely until gnocchi are deeply puffed and crispy and sausage edges are caramelized.
  5. Toss with Pesto & Parmesan: Remove sheet pan from oven. Immediately dollop and drizzle basil pesto across the hot gnocchi and sausage, then sprinkle generously with grated parmesan. Toss gently on the pan, scatter fresh torn basil on top, and serve piping hot straight from the sheet pan!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Never boil your gnocchi before putting them on the sheet pan! Boiling floods potato gnocchi with water, turning them mushy in the oven. Roasting them bone-dry right out of the package allows the exterior to fry crisp in olive oil while the interior steams into a light, pillowy cloud.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use raw bulk Italian sausage instead of pre-cooked?
Yes! Pinch raw sausage into bite-sized marble balls and scatter across the sheet pan. They cook through completely in 15 minutes alongside the gnocchi.
Can I use cauliflower gnocchi?
Yes! Trader Joe's frozen cauliflower gnocchi works wonderfully—toss with olive oil straight from frozen and roast at 425°F for 20 minutes until crisp.
How do I reheat leftover roasted gnocchi?
Reheat in an air fryer or dry skillet at 375°F for 4 minutes to restore the crispy crust. Microwaving will make the gnocchi soft and chewy.
