Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sticky honey sesame chicken bites, toss 1.5 lbs of cubed chicken breasts in cornstarch, salt, and pepper, then shallow-fry in 2 tablespoons of oil over medium-high heat until golden and crispy (6–8 minutes). Whisk together honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, minced garlic, ginger, toasted sesame oil, and a tiny cornstarch slurry. Simmer the sauce for 2 minutes until syrupy, toss in the chicken to coat, and garnish with sesame seeds and scallions.

Why This Beats Takeout Every Time

  • No Deep-Fryer Needed: Cornstarch creates a shattering crisp exterior with just 2 tablespoons of shallow skillet oil.
  • Real Clover Honey: Takeout versions rely on high-fructose corn syrup; this recipe uses pure honey and fresh ginger for pure aromatic flavor.
  • Under 20 Minutes: From cutting the chicken to placing bowls on the table takes less time than waiting for delivery drivers.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Coat in Cornstarch: Pat diced chicken completely dry with paper towels. Season with salt and pepper, then toss in a bowl with 1/3 cup cornstarch until evenly coated.
  2. Sear to Crisp: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken in a single layer without crowding. Cook 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy (165°F). Transfer to a plate.
  3. Simmer Honey Glaze: Lower heat to medium. Add garlic and ginger; sauté 30 seconds. Whisk in honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and the cornstarch slurry. Simmer 1–2 minutes until thick and syrupy.
  4. Toss & Glaze: Immediately toss the crispy chicken into the bubbling sauce for 60 seconds until glistening and evenly coated.
  5. Garnish: Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve immediately over hot jasmine rice!

Elena’s Cornstarch Crisping Rule

Dry Chicken Equals Shattering Crunch: Moisture is the enemy of crispiness! Always pat your raw chicken cubes thoroughly dry with paper towels before tossing them in cornstarch. This ensures the starch forms a paper-thin, crackly shell when hitting hot oil instead of turning gummy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you keep the chicken crispy once tossed in honey glaze?
The key is tossing the chicken in the sauce immediately before serving! Do not simmer the chicken in the sauce for an extended time. Tossing for just 60 seconds coats the crispy cornstarch crust while preserving that satisfying restaurant crunch.
Can I make these honey sesame chicken bites in an air fryer?
Yes! Spray the cornstarch-coated chicken cubes lightly with oil spray and air-fry at 400°F (200°C) for 10 to 12 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through. Then toss with the warmed stovetop honey sauce.
How can I make this recipe gluten-free?
Simply swap the soy sauce for certified gluten-free tamari or coconut aminos. Cornstarch is naturally gluten-free and creates the signature crispy crust without wheat flour.
