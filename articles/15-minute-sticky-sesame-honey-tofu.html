Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy sticky sesame honey tofu, press 1 block (14 oz) extra-firm tofu dry and cut into 3/4-inch cubes. Toss cubes with 2 tbsp cornstarch and 1/2 tsp salt until evenly coated. Pan-fry in 2 tbsp neutral oil over medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes, turning until all sides are crunchy and golden brown. In a small bowl, whisk 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 minced garlic cloves, and 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water. Pour glaze over sizzling tofu and toss 60 seconds until thick and sticky.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Cornstarch Crisp Shield: Tossing pressed extra-firm tofu cubes in cornstarch creates a shatteringly crisp outer skin that retains its crunch under hot sauce.
  • Quick Press Technique: Squeezing tofu between paper towels with a heavy skillet for 3 minutes removes enough moisture to achieve instant golden browning.
  • Sticky Honey-Soy Caramelization: Simmering honey with sesame oil and soy sauce creates a restaurant-style lacquer that clings to every crevice.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Press & Cube Tofu: Drain tofu and wrap tightly in a clean kitchen towel or double layer of paper towels. Press gently with a heavy skillet on top for 3 minutes to remove surface moisture. Cut into 3/4-inch bite-sized cubes.
  2. Dust in Cornstarch: In a medium mixing bowl, toss tofu cubes with 1.5 tbsp cornstarch and 1/2 tsp salt until every cube has a light, powdery white coating.
  3. Pan-Fry to Shattering Crisp: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wide non-stick skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add tofu cubes in a single layer with space between them. Fry undisturbed for 3 minutes until bottom is golden and crunchy. Turn cubes and cook for 3 to 4 more minutes until golden on all sides.
  4. Whisk Honey Sesame Glaze: In a small cup, whisk soy sauce, honey, toasted sesame oil, rice vinegar, grated garlic, grated ginger, the remaining 1/2 tsp cornstarch, and 2 tbsp water until blended.
  5. Glaze & Lacquer Tofu: Pour the glaze directly over the hot crispy tofu in the skillet. Toss continuously with a spatula for 60 seconds as the glaze bubbles furiously and transforms into a sticky, glossy lacquer coating every cube. Remove from heat immediately.
  6. Assemble Bowls & Garnish: Scoop warm jasmine rice into bowls, nestle steamed broccoli florets alongside, and crown with heaps of sticky sesame tofu. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and sliced scallions. Serve right away!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Toss the tofu in the bubbling glaze for no more than 60 seconds! Lingering in simmering sauce will soften the crispy cornstarch crust. Coating it fast over high heat keeps the interior fluffy, the exterior crunch intact, and the glaze sticky and sweet.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make this recipe 100% vegan?
Simply swap the honey with pure maple syrup, agave nectar, or brown sugar. The sauce retains the identical glossy sticky consistency!
Can I make this in the air fryer?
Yes! Air fry the cornstarch-coated tofu cubes at 400°F (200°C) for 12 to 14 minutes, shaking halfway through. Toss in warm simmering glaze in a bowl.
How do I store and reheat leftovers?
Store leftover glazed tofu in an airtight container for up to 3 days in the fridge. To restore crispiness, reheat in an air fryer at 375°F for 4 minutes.
