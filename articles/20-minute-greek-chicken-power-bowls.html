Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute Greek chicken power bowls, slice chicken breasts into thin cutlets and toss with olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, minced garlic, and oregano. Sear in a hot skillet for 3–4 minutes per side until golden. Slice the chicken and arrange over fluffy quinoa and baby spinach with diced cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, Kalamata olives, crumbled feta cheese, and a generous drizzle of cool cucumber dill tzatziki.

Why Mediterranean Power Bowls Rule Dinner

  • 40g Lean Protein Fuel: Packed with clean grilled poultry and protein-rich quinoa to fuel workouts and busy workdays.
  • Warm & Cool Contrast: The combination of hot juicy lemon chicken against chilled, crisp cucumbers and cold tzatziki creates amazing texture.
  • Ultimate Meal-Prep Friendly: Holds up crisp and flavorful in lunch containers for up to 4 days.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Quick Marinade: Toss sliced chicken cutlets with 1 tbsp olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  2. Sear Chicken: Heat remaining olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear cutlets 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through (165°F). Slice into strips.
  3. Build Bowls: Divide fresh spinach and warm cooked quinoa among 4 serving bowls.
  4. Arrange Toppings: Top each bowl with sliced warm chicken, diced cucumbers, halved tomatoes, and Kalamata olives.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Sprinkle with crumbled feta cheese and fresh dill. Dollop with cool tzatziki and serve with lemon wedges!

Elena’s Cutlet Searing Secret

Cut into Thin Cutlets for Speed: Thick whole chicken breasts take 15–20 minutes to cook through in a skillet and frequently dry out. Slicing breasts horizontally into thin cutlets cuts searing time down to just 3 to 4 minutes per side, sealing in natural juices while creating gorgeous charred golden edges!

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do these Greek chicken power bowls last for meal prep?
These bowls stay exceptionally fresh in airtight meal prep containers in the refrigerator for up to 4 days! Keep the tzatziki sauce in small separate condiment cups so the cucumbers and spinach remain crisp.
What grain works best for Mediterranean bowls?
Quinoa is ideal because it adds 8g of complete plant protein per cup and has a nutty, light texture. Brown rice, farro, couscous, or cauliflower rice (for low-carb) are all fantastic bases.
Can I serve this bowl cold?
Yes! The chicken tastes incredible either warm straight from the skillet or chilled from the fridge, making this one of the best grab-and-go lunch solutions.
