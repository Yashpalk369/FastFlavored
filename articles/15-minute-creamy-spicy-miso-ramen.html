15-Minute Creamy Spicy Miso Ramen with Jammy Soft-Boiled Egg

Tokyo ramen shop quality in 15 minutes: springy noodles swimming in a rich, creamy white miso and sesame chili broth with sweet buttered corn, baby spinach, and a runny soft-boiled egg.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute creamy spicy miso ramen, heat 1 tbsp sesame oil in a pot over medium heat. Sauté 3 minced garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger for 45 seconds. Whisk in 3 tbsp white or yellow miso paste, 2 tbsp tahini (or unsweetened creamy peanut butter), 1 tbsp chili oil or chili crisp, 1 tbsp soy sauce, and 1 tsp sugar until smooth. Pour in 5 cups rich chicken bone broth (or vegetable broth) and 1/2 cup unsweetened soy milk or oat milk (this creates the signature cloudy, velvety ramen emulsion). Bring to a gentle simmer for 3 minutes. In a separate pot of boiling water, cook 4 packs of fresh or instant ramen noodles (discard seasoning packets) for 2 to 3 minutes; drain. Divide noodles into bowls, ladle hot creamy miso broth over them, and top with warm sweet corn, baby spinach, sliced green scallions, toasted sesame seeds, and a halved jammy soft-boiled egg.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Soy Milk & Tahini Broth Emulsion: Whisking a splash of unsweetened soy milk and sesame tahini into hot chicken broth mimics the rich, collagen-heavy mouthfeel of 12-hour tonkotsu broth in 3 minutes.
  • Gentle Miso Dissolution: Never boil miso paste vigorously; dissolving miso over gentle heat preserves active live enzymes, aromatic fragrance, and prevents graininess.
  • Separate Water Noodle Boil: Boiling ramen noodles in plain salted water rather than directly in the broth prevents starchy cloudiness from muddying the pristine miso broth.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil Noodles: Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil. Drop in ramen noodles and cook according to package instructions (about 2 to 3 minutes until chewy). Drain in a colander, rinse briefly with warm water to remove excess surface starch, and set aside.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: In a separate soup pot or Dutch oven, heat sesame oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and grated ginger; sauté for 45 seconds until fragrant without browning.
  3. Whisk Miso & Tahini Paste: Reduce heat to low. Add white miso paste, tahini, chili oil, soy sauce, and brown sugar. Whisk continuously for 60 seconds into a smooth, fragrant aromatic paste.
  4. Simmer Creamy Broth: Gradually pour in the chicken bone broth and unsweetened soy milk while whisking vigorously until the miso paste fully dissolves. Bring to a gentle bubbling simmer over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes until velvety and steaming hot (do not hard-boil).
  5. Assemble Bowls: Divide cooked noodles among 4 deep ramen bowls. Ladle the hot creamy spicy miso broth generously over the noodles. Arrange sweet corn, baby spinach, green scallions, and a halved jammy egg on top. Drizzle with extra chili oil and shower with sesame seeds!

Elena’s 6.5-Minute Jammy Egg Recipe

For genuine ramen-shop jammy eggs: lower cold eggs straight from the fridge into boiling water. Boil for exactly 6 minutes and 30 seconds, then immediately plunge into an ice water bath for 5 minutes. The whites are completely set and tender while the yolk stays thick, golden, and gooey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this vegetarian or vegan?
Yes! Use rich vegetable stock, plant-based unsweetened soy milk, and replace the soft-boiled egg with crispy pan-seared cubed tofu.
How spicy is this broth?
It has a comforting medium warmth from the chili oil. You can dial it down by using 1/2 tbsp chili oil or boost it with extra chili crisp and sriracha.
What are the best noodles to buy?
Look for fresh refrigerated Japanese ramen noodles (such as Sun Noodle or Myojo) in the produce or Asian refrigerated section; they have incredible chew and elasticity.
