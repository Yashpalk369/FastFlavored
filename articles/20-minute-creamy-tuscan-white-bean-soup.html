Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make restorative 20-minute Tuscan white bean soup, sauté minced garlic and Italian herbs in olive oil, then add sun-dried tomatoes, 1 can of cannellini beans, and broth. Mash a second can of cannellini beans and stir into the broth—this releases natural bean starches that thicken the soup into a velvety cream without heavy cream. Finish with fresh baby kale, grated parmesan, and lemon juice.

Why This Soup Is a Weeknight Superstar

  • Creamy Without Heavy Cream: Naturally rich and thick thanks to blended cannellini beans, keeping the soup light and gut-friendly.
  • Pantry Powerhouse: Made almost entirely from canned white beans, sun-dried tomatoes, and broth you can keep in stock at all times.
  • 11g Fiber & 16g Protein: Deeply nourishing and filling without needing meat, though sausage or bacon pairs easily.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sauté Aromatics: In a 4-quart Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat. Sauté garlic, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes for 45 seconds.
  2. Simmer: Add sun-dried tomatoes, 1 can of whole cannellini beans, and 4 cups of broth. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Mash & Thicken: Mash the second can of beans with 1/2 cup broth in a bowl until smooth. Stir the purée into the soup pot.
  4. Wilt Greens: Add baby kale and grated parmesan. Simmer for 5 minutes until kale is tender and soup is thick and velvety.
  5. Finish & Serve: Stir in lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper. Ladle into bowls with a drizzle of olive oil and crusty bread.

Elena’s Bean-Mashing Secret

Don't Dirty a Blender: You do not need a food processor or immersion blender to get that silky Tuscan texture! Simply crush half the beans in a shallow bowl with the back of a fork or potato masher. The rustic texture gives the soup authentic trattoria character with zero cleanup hassle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you make white bean soup creamy without heavy cream?
The authentic Italian secret is mashing one can of cannellini beans into a coarse purée before adding it to the broth. The natural starches in the beans dissolve into the simmering soup, giving you a luscious, velvety texture with zero heavy cream or butter!
Can I add sausage or bacon to this soup?
Yes! Brown 1/2 pound of Italian sausage or chopped pancetta in the pot before adding the garlic. It adds incredible savory depth and smokiness to the broth.
