Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute crispy garlic parmesan crusted pork chops, dip seasoned 1-inch boneless pork chops in beaten egg, then press firmly into a mixture of grated parmesan cheese, panko breadcrumbs, garlic powder, and Italian herbs. Sear in olive oil over medium heat for 4–5 minutes until golden. Flip and baste with 3 tablespoons of foaming butter, smashed garlic, and fresh rosemary until the internal temperature reaches 145°F (63°C). Rest 3 minutes before serving.

Why These Pork Chops Never Turn Out Dry

  • Parmesan Crust Insulator: The cheese and panko crust acts as a thermal shield, locking moisture inside while crisping on the skillet surface.
  • Steakhouse Butter Basting: Spooning foaming garlic butter continuously over the top cooks the meat gently from above without overcooking the bottom crust.
  • The 145°F Sweet Spot: Modern USDA guidelines recommend cooking pork to 145°F with a 3-minute rest, resulting in tender, slightly blush, succulent meat.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Dry & Season: Pat pork chops completely dry with paper towels. Season both sides with kosher salt, black pepper, and smoked paprika.
  2. Coat in Crust: Mix parmesan, panko, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning on a plate. Dip chops in beaten egg, then press firmly into the parmesan mixture on both sides.
  3. Sear First Side: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook chops undisturbed for 4–5 minutes until bottom crust is deeply golden.
  4. Flip & Butter Baste: Flip chops. Add butter, smashed garlic, and rosemary to the skillet. Continuously spoon foaming butter over the chops for 4–5 minutes until internal temp reaches 140°F–145°F.
  5. Rest & Serve: Transfer chops to a cutting board to rest for 3 minutes. Squeeze fresh lemon juice on top, drizzle with pan butter, and enjoy!

Elena’s Butter-Basting Rule

The Arrosé (Butter-Baste) Technique: When searing cheese-crusted meats, high direct contact on both sides can burn the cheese before the center is cooked. By flipping and immediately basting with foaming rosemary garlic butter, you envelop the top crust in rich flavor while gently finishing the cook without scorching!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you keep pork chops from drying out in the skillet?
Use thick-cut chops (at least 1 inch thick) and cook only until an internal meat thermometer reads 140°F–145°F. Basting with foaming garlic butter keeps the exterior moist while sealing in natural juices.
Can I make these pork chops gluten-free or keto?
Yes! Swap the panko breadcrumbs for finely crushed pork rinds or almond flour, or use 100% grated parmesan cheese for the crust. It crisps up beautifully with zero carbs.
Can this be made in an air fryer?
Yes! Preheat your air fryer to 390°F (198°C). Spray the coated chops lightly with avocado oil spray and cook for 10 to 12 minutes, flipping halfway through, until internal temperature reaches 145°F.
