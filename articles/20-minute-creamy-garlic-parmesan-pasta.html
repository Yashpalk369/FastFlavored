Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy garlic parmesan pasta, boil fettuccine until al dente while searing sliced cremini mushrooms in butter and olive oil until deeply golden. Add minced garlic, 3/4 cup heavy cream, and 1/2 cup broth, simmering for 3 minutes before taking off the heat to melt in 3/4 cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano. Emulsify with 3 tablespoons of starchy pasta water for a glossy, restaurant-grade coat.

Why This Cream Sauce Never Separates

  • Low Heat Melting: Fresh parmesan is folded into the cream sauce off the boil, preventing oily separation or graininess.
  • Pasta Water Emulsion: Starch-rich cooking water binds fat and liquid together into a velvety glaze that clings to every noodle.
  • Rich Mushroom Umami: Caramelizing mushrooms first builds deep savory flavor without needing meat.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil Pasta: Cook pasta in salted water until al dente. Save 1/2 cup pasta cooking water, then drain.
  2. Sear Mushrooms: In a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat, melt 2 tbsp butter with olive oil. Sear mushrooms for 5 minutes until deeply browned.
  3. Simmer Aromatics: Lower heat to medium. Add minced garlic for 45 seconds. Pour in broth and heavy cream; simmer gently for 3 minutes.
  4. Melt Cheese: Turn heat to low. Stir in remaining 1 tbsp butter and grated parmesan until velvety and smooth. Season with salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
  5. Toss & Emulsify: Add pasta and parsley into skillet. Toss vigorously, adding 2 to 3 tbsp pasta water to create a glossy emulsion. Serve hot!

Elena’s Starchy Pasta Water Secret

Liquid Gold in the Sauce: Never dump all your pasta water down the drain! The starch released from the boiling noodles acts as an all-natural emulsifier. Just 2 to 3 tablespoons added to the cream and cheese turns a loose sauce into a rich, silky glaze that hugs the pasta without clumping.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you prevent cream sauce from separating or curdling?
Keep the heat low when adding dairy and cheese! Never let the sauce come to a violent rolling boil once the parmesan is added, and use freshly grated cheese rather than pre-shredded bagged cheese which contains anti-caking starches.
Can I add chicken or shrimp to this pasta?
Yes! Sliced chicken breast cutlets or peeled shrimp can be quickly pan-seared in the skillet before cooking the mushrooms, then stirred back into the finished cream sauce.
