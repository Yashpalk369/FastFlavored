20-Minute Sheet-Pan Basil Pesto Salmon with Roasted Asparagus

Effortless Mediterranean elegance in 20 minutes: center-cut salmon fillets slathered with vibrant basil pesto, roasted on a single sheet pan with fresh green asparagus, cherry tomatoes, and lemon.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan basil pesto salmon with asparagus, preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Trim woody ends from 1 lb fresh green asparagus. Toss asparagus and 1 cup cherry tomatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper; arrange on a large rimmed baking sheet. Place 4 center-cut salmon fillets (6 oz each) in the center of the sheet pan. Season salmon with a pinch of salt, then spread 1.5 tbsp prepared basil pesto evenly over the top of each fillet, pressing gently into a vibrant green crust. Lay lemon wheels across the pan. Roast at 425°F for 12 to 14 minutes until salmon flakes easily with a fork (135°F internal temperature) and asparagus is tender-crisp with roasted tips. Finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Pesto Moisture Blanket: A generous spread of basil pesto forms an aromatic herbal barrier that locks in natural salmon juices and prevents the delicate fish from drying out in the oven.
  • Synchronized 14-Minute Roasting: Fresh asparagus spears and 1-inch thick salmon fillets cook to tender-crisp perfection in the exact same 12-to-14-minute window at 425°F.
  • Nutrient-Dense Mediterranean Profile: Packed with healthy heart-friendly omega-3 fatty acids, vibrant green antioxidants, and 38g of clean protein with virtually zero cleanup.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat & Prep Sheet Pan: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper or foil for easy cleanup.
  2. Season Vegetables: Place trimmed asparagus spears and whole cherry tomatoes on the baking sheet. Drizzle with 1.5 tbsp olive oil, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper. Toss with hands and push to the outer edges of the pan.
  3. Season & Pesto-Crust Salmon: Place the salmon fillets skin-side down in the center of the baking sheet. Sprinkle each fillet with a pinch of salt. Spoon approximately 1.5 tbsp basil pesto over the top of each fillet, spreading evenly to create a thick green crust. Scatter lemon wheels across the pan.
  4. Roast to Flaky Perfection: Roast at 425°F for 12 to 14 minutes until the salmon is opaque, flakes easily with a fork, and reaches 130°F–135°F internal temperature, while asparagus is tender-crisp with roasted tips.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Remove sheet pan from oven. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the hot salmon and vegetables. Scatter with fresh basil leaves and a dusting of parmesan cheese. Serve immediately straight from the pan!

Elena’s Refrigerated Pesto Secret

Always buy fresh basil pesto from the refrigerated pasta section rather than the dusty canned sauce aisle! Refrigerated pesto uses fresh unpasteurized basil, real extra virgin olive oil, and aged parmesan, keeping a bright emerald green color and intoxicating fragrance that elevates simple salmon to five-star status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this with frozen salmon?
Yes! Thaw salmon fillets completely in the refrigerator, and press thoroughly dry with paper towels before slathering with pesto so the sauce doesn't slide off.
Can I use white fish instead?
Cod loins, halibut, or sea bass bake magnificently with pesto—adjust bake time to 12 minutes for 1-inch thick fillets.
How do I store leftovers?
Store cooked salmon and asparagus in an airtight glass container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently in a 350°F oven or toaster oven for 5 minutes.
