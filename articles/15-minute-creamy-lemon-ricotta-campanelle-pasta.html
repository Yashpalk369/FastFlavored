15-Minute Creamy Lemon Ricotta Campanelle Pasta with Sweet Peas

Bright, delicate Italian vegetarian comfort in 15 minutes: fluted flower-shaped campanelle pasta tossed in creamy lemon-whipped ricotta, tender sweet peas, parmesan, and fresh mint.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute creamy lemon ricotta campanelle pasta, boil 10 oz campanelle (or fusilli) in salted water until al dente (save 1 cup starchy pasta cooking water before draining). During the final 2 minutes of pasta boiling, drop 1 cup frozen sweet peas directly into the boiling pasta pot. Drain pasta and peas together. In a large bowl or directly in the warm pot off-heat, whisk 1 cup whole-milk ricotta cheese with 1/2 cup hot pasta water, 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese, 2 tbsp olive oil, zest and juice of 1 large lemon, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp black pepper until smooth and velvety. Toss the hot pasta and peas through the ricotta cream until every fluted petal is coated. Shower with fresh torn mint leaves, lemon zest, and extra grated parmesan.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Campanelle Cone Pockets: The ruffled flower-cone shape of campanelle (gigli) catches creamy ricotta sauce and tender sweet peas inside its delicate fluted petals.
  • Two-Minute Pea Blanch Shortcut: Tossing frozen peas directly into the boiling pasta during the last 120 seconds cooks them to sweet, vibrant green pop without dirtying another pot.
  • Off-Heat Ricotta Whisking: Ricotta should never be boiled; whisking cold ricotta with hot starchy pasta water off the heat creates a velvety emulsion that never curdles.

Ingredients

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil Campanelle & Peas: Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add campanelle pasta and cook until al dente (about 8–9 minutes). During the final 2 minutes of boiling, drop frozen sweet peas directly into the boiling pot. Before draining, ladle out 1 cup of hot starchy pasta water. Drain pasta and peas in a colander.
  2. Whisk Velvet Lemon Ricotta: In the warm empty pasta pot (off the heat), combine whole-milk ricotta, olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, grated parmesan cheese, salt, black pepper, and 1/2 cup of the hot reserved pasta water. Whisk vigorously for 45 seconds until silky, glossy, and completely smooth.
  3. Toss Pasta & Peas: Tumble the hot drained campanelle pasta and sweet peas directly into the lemon ricotta cream. Toss gently with a wooden spoon for 60 seconds, adding an extra splash of hot pasta water if needed until every ruffled cone is coated in a velvety sheen.
  4. Garnish & Serve: Divide into warm bowls. Top each serving with fresh torn mint leaves, freshly cracked black pepper, extra grated parmesan, and a final drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. Serve immediately!

Elena’s Whole-Milk Ricotta Mandate

Always check the tub to ensure you are buying WHOLE-MILK ricotta! Part-skim ricotta has high moisture and gums that turn watery and grainy when mixed with hot pasta. Whole-milk ricotta has rich butterfat that emulsifies with hot pasta water into pure restaurant liquid silk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add protein to this pasta?
Yes! Sautéed jumbo shrimp, crispy crumbled prosciutto, or shredded rotisserie chicken breast pair magnificently with lemon ricotta.
Can I use basil instead of mint?
Yes! Fresh sweet basil or fresh dill make wonderful herbal substitutes for mint.
How do I store leftovers?
Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days in the fridge. Reheat gently on the stovetop over low heat with a splash of milk or water to revive the creamy sauce.
