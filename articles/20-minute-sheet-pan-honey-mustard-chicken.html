20-Minute Sheet-Pan Crispy Honey Mustard Chicken Thighs & Brussels Sprouts

Golden crispy boneless chicken thighs and caramelized halved Brussels sprouts roasted on a sheet pan, basted in a glossy honey dijon glaze with bacon crumbles in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute sheet-pan honey mustard chicken, whisk 1/4 cup honey, 2 tbsp Dijon mustard, 1 tbsp whole grain mustard, 2 tbsp olive oil, 3 minced garlic cloves, salt, and pepper. Toss 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs and 1 lb trimmed, halved Brussels sprouts in the glaze. Spread on a rimmed baking sheet in a single layer and scatter 3 slices of chopped thick-cut bacon across the pan. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 15 minutes, then broil on High for 2 minutes until chicken skin is blistered and caramelized and Brussels sprouts are crispy.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Cut-Side Down Brussels Sprout Sear: Placing Brussels sprouts cut-side down against hot metal caramelizes natural plant sugars into deeply browned, nutty chips in 15 minutes.
  • Dual-Mustard Honey Glaze: Blending sharp Dijon with stone-ground mustard seeds gives the honey glaze texture and a balanced sweet-tangy flavor.
  • Bacon Fat Roasting Alchemy: Rendered bacon drippings coat the Brussels sprouts during roasting, crisping their outer leaves without frying.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat Oven & Sheet Pan: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Place a large rimmed baking sheet on the middle oven rack while preheating.
  2. Whisk Honey Mustard Glaze: In a medium bowl, whisk honey, Dijon mustard, whole grain mustard, olive oil, minced garlic, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper until emulsified.
  3. Toss Chicken & Brussels Sprouts: In a large bowl, toss halved Brussels sprouts and chicken thighs with the honey mustard glaze, reserving 2 tablespoons of glaze for post-bake brushing. Add chopped raw bacon pieces.
  4. Arrange on Hot Sheet Pan: Carefully pull the hot baking sheet from the oven. Spread chicken thighs and Brussels sprouts in a single layer, ensuring Brussels sprouts are placed cut-side down against the hot metal for optimal browning.
  5. Roast & Char-Broil: Roast at 425°F for 14 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F. Brush chicken with the reserved 2 tbsp glaze and broil on High for 2 minutes until chicken is bubbly and lacquered and Brussels sprout edges are crispy and charred. Garnish with fresh thyme and serve immediately!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Always arrange your Brussels sprouts CUT-SIDE DOWN directly touching the baking sheet! The direct metal contact traps heat and renders natural vegetable sugars into crispy, golden-brown caramelized chips that taste like gourmet candy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this without bacon?
Yes! Simply omit the bacon and toss the Brussels sprouts with an additional 1/2 tbsp of olive oil.
Can I use frozen Brussels sprouts?
Fresh Brussels sprouts are strongly recommended. Frozen Brussels sprouts release excess water that steams rather than roasts crisp.
How do I store and reheat leftovers?
Store in an airtight container for up to 4 days. Reheat in an air fryer or toaster oven at 380°F for 4 minutes to restore the crispy Brussels sprout leaves.
