15-Minute Spicy Garlic Chili Crisp Sesame Udon Noodles

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute spicy garlic sesame udon, boil frozen sanuki udon noodles for 1 minute (or soak vacuum-packed udon in hot water) and drain. In a skillet over medium heat, sizzle 4 minced garlic cloves and 2 sliced scallions in 1.5 tbsp toasted sesame oil for 90 seconds until fragrant. Stir in 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp chili crisp, 1 tbsp oyster sauce (or hoisin), 1 tsp rice vinegar, and 1 tsp brown sugar. Toss noodles directly in the bubbling sauce for 2 minutes with 2 tbsp noodle water until glossy. Top with a jammy egg and toasted sesame seeds.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Frozen Sanuki Udon Supremacy: Frozen udon cakes maintain the quintessential springy chew (koshi) and thaw in 60 seconds without mushiness.
  • Aromatic Garlic Chili Sizzle: Blooming minced garlic directly in toasted sesame oil before adding liquids extracts maximum aroma without burning.
  • Starchy Water Emulsion: Splashing 2 tablespoons of hot starchy udon cooking water into the soy-chili sauce creates an ultra-glossy glaze that hugs every noodle.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Flash-Boil Udon Noodles: Bring a pot of water to a rapid boil. Drop in frozen Sanuki udon cakes and cook for 60 to 90 seconds, gently loosening with chopsticks until noodles separate. Reserve 1/4 cup cooking water, then drain immediately.
  2. Sizzle Aromatics in Sesame Oil: Heat 1.5 tbsp toasted sesame oil in a wide skillet or wok over medium heat. Add minced garlic and the white parts of the sliced scallions. Sizzle for 60 to 90 seconds until fragrant and golden (watch closely so garlic doesn't scorch).
  3. Whisk Umami Chili Sauce: Add low-sodium soy sauce, dark soy sauce, chili crisp, oyster sauce, rice vinegar, and brown sugar into the skillet. Swirl together until bubbling vigorously.
  4. Gloss & Toss Noodles: Add the drained udon noodles and 2 tablespoons of hot cooking water into the skillet. Toss continuously with tongs for 2 minutes over medium-high heat until noodles absorb the sauce and turn dark, glossy, and lacquered.
  5. Plate, Garnish & Crown with Egg: Divide steaming noodles into two deep bowls. Place a jammy soft-boiled egg half on top, scatter green scallion slices and toasted sesame seeds generously over each bowl. Serve hot!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Never skip the starchy noodle cooking water! A two-tablespoon splash combines with the sesame oil and chili crisp fats to emulsify into a silky, glossy coating that clings tightly to the thick udon instead of pooling in the bottom of your bowl.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a 6-minute jammy egg?
Lower fridge-cold eggs into boiling water, boil for exactly 6 minutes and 30 seconds, then immediately transfer into an ice water bath for 5 minutes before peeling.
Can I make this vegetarian or vegan?
Yes! Just use mushroom-based vegetarian oyster sauce (or hoisin sauce). The rest of the ingredients are completely plant-based.
How spicy is this recipe?
With 1 tablespoon of chili crisp, it has a pleasant mild-medium tingling heat. Feel free to dial it up to 2 tablespoons for serious chili lovers.
