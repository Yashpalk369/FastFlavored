15-Minute Creamy Garlic Florentine Tilapia Skillet with Spinach

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute creamy Florentine tilapia, pat 4 tilapia fillets (approx. 5 oz each) bone-dry and season both sides with Italian herbs, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear tilapia undisturbed for 3 minutes per side until golden brown and flaky; transfer to a plate. Reduce heat to medium, add 1 tbsp butter, 4 minced garlic cloves, and 1/3 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes; sauté for 60 seconds. Pour in 1/2 cup chicken or vegetable broth, 3/4 cup heavy cream, and 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese, simmering for 2 minutes until bubbly and thickened. Stir in 3 packed cups of fresh baby spinach until wilted. Return tilapia to the skillet, spooning the velvety sauce over the fillets for 60 seconds. Finish with fresh dill and lemon wedges.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Delicate White Fish Timing: Tilapia is thin and tender; searing for 3 minutes per side prevents overcooking and keeps the delicate white flakes moist and succulent.
  • Florentine Cream Body: Fresh baby spinach and parmesan cheese thicken heavy cream into a rich sauce that clings effortlessly to mild white fish fillets.
  • Sun-Dried Tomato Acidity: Piquant sun-dried tomatoes cut through the butter cream, adding vibrant color and sweet savory notes.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep & Season Fish: Pat tilapia fillets completely dry on both sides with paper towels. Season with Italian herbs, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Sear Tilapia Fillets: Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a wide stainless steel or cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Gently lay tilapia fillets into the hot skillet. Sear undisturbed for 3 minutes until a light golden crust forms. Carefully flip with a thin fish spatula and sear for another 2.5 to 3 minutes until fish flakes easily. Transfer fillets to a plate.
  3. Sauté Garlic & Tomatoes: Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining 1 tbsp butter, minced garlic, and sliced sun-dried tomatoes to the skillet drippings. Sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant without browning.
  4. Simmer Parmesan Cream: Pour in chicken broth, heavy cream, and grated parmesan cheese. Whisk continuously for 2 minutes as the sauce bubbles into a rich, velvety consistency.
  5. Wilt Spinach & Combine: Stir fresh baby spinach into the simmering sauce for 60 seconds until wilted and tender. Nestle seared tilapia fillets back into the skillet. Spoon the creamy Florentine sauce over the fish and simmer for 60 seconds to reheat. Garnish with fresh dill and serve with lemon wedges!

Elena’s Gentle Flip Technique

Because tilapia has fine, delicate muscle fibers without thick connective tissue, standard tongs will snap the fillets in half! Always use a wide, flexible metal spatula, slide it completely underneath the center of the fillet, and gently guide the fish over in one confident motion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen tilapia?
Yes! Thaw completely in cold water for 15 minutes, then press firmly between paper towels to remove all excess water before searing.
What sides pair best with Florentine tilapia?
This creamy dish is fantastic served over buttered noodles, garlic mashed potatoes, steamed rice, or alongside roasted asparagus.
Is this dish keto friendly?
Yes! At only 6g net carbs and 34g protein, it is an outstanding high-protein keto and low-carb dinner.
