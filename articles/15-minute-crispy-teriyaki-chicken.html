15-Minute Crispy Teriyaki Chicken Cutlet Bowls

Sliced golden crispy pan-fried chicken cutlets glazed in a glossy homemade teriyaki sauce over jasmine rice with steamed broccoli in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute crispy teriyaki chicken bowls, dust 4 thin chicken cutlets (1.25 lbs) in 2 tbsp cornstarch, salt, and pepper. Pan-fry in 2 tbsp vegetable oil over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes per side until deeply golden and crunchy; transfer to a cutting board and slice. In the skillet, whisk 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce, 3 tbsp mirin, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp grated ginger, and 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water; bubble for 60 seconds until a thick glossy teriyaki glaze forms. Arrange chicken over jasmine rice and broccoli, and drizzle glaze on top.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Cornstarch Crust Lock: Dusting chicken cutlets in cornstarch creates a shatteringly crisp barrier that prevents teriyaki glaze from softening the chicken.
  • Authentic Mirin Glaze Shine: Using Japanese mirin alongside soy sauce and brown sugar produces a mirror-like lacquer that commercial bottled sauces cannot replicate.
  • Drizzle Don't Simmer: Drizzling thickened teriyaki sauce over pre-sliced crispy cutlets preserves maximum crunch while coating every tender fiber.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Dredge & Crisp Chicken Cutlets: Pat chicken cutlets dry with paper towels; season lightly with salt and pepper. Dust both sides evenly with 2 tbsp cornstarch. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add chicken cutlets and fry for 3 to 4 minutes per side until deeply golden and crunchy (internal temp 165°F). Transfer to a cutting board and let rest 2 minutes, then slice into 1/2-inch strips.
  2. Wipe Skillet & Whisk Glaze: Wipe any excess oil from the skillet with a folded paper towel. Add soy sauce, mirin, brown sugar, minced garlic, and grated ginger. Bring to a rapid simmer over medium heat for 60 seconds until sugar dissolves.
  3. Thicken into Mirror Glaze: Whisk the cornstarch slurry and pour directly into the bubbling skillet. Whisk constantly for 45 to 60 seconds as the sauce transforms into a rich, glossy, mahogany teriyaki lacquer. Remove from heat immediately.
  4. Assemble Bowls: Scoop warm jasmine rice into bowls and arrange steamed broccoli florets on one side. Fan out sliced crispy chicken cutlets on top of the rice.
  5. Glaze & Garnish: Ladle the hot glossy teriyaki glaze generously over the crispy chicken and rice. Shower with toasted sesame seeds and sliced green scallions. Serve immediately while chicken is piping hot and crunchy!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Slice your crispy chicken cutlets BEFORE spooning the teriyaki sauce over them! Pouring the sauce over pre-sliced pieces ensures the glaze seeps between every juicy cut while keeping the crispy golden breading shatteringly crunchy on top.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I substitute for mirin?
If you don't have mirin, substitute 3 tbsp water mixed with 1 tbsp rice vinegar and 1 tsp extra sugar or honey.
Can I use chicken thighs?
Yes! Boneless skinless chicken thighs work brilliantly—sear for 4–5 minutes per side until golden and cooked to 170°F.
How do I reheat leftover teriyaki chicken?
Reheat chicken slices in an air fryer or toaster oven at 375°F for 3–4 minutes to restore crispiness, then drizzle with remaining glaze.
