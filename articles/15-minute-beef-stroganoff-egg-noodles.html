15-Minute Creamy Garlic Butter Beef Stroganoff with Egg Noodles

Tender seared beef sirloin strips and cremini mushrooms simmered in a luscious sour cream and dijon beef gravy tossed with wide egg noodles in 15 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 15-minute beef stroganoff, boil 8 oz wide egg noodles for 6 minutes and drain. Slice 1.25 lbs top sirloin steak into thin 1/2-inch strips. Sear in 1 tbsp oil in a smoking-hot skillet for 2 minutes until browned on the edges; remove to a plate. In the same skillet, melt 2 tbsp butter and sauté 8 oz sliced cremini mushrooms and 3 minced garlic cloves for 3 minutes. Whisk in 1 cup beef broth, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, and 1/2 cup sour cream off high heat until velvety. Return beef and accumulated juices, toss with hot egg noodles, and garnish with fresh dill.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Fast Sirloin Strip Sear: Flash-searing thin sirloin strips for only 2 minutes prevents beef from turning tough and rubbery while developing savory pan fond.
  • Mushroom Caramelization Depth: Sautéing cremini mushrooms in butter until deeply browned releases natural glutamate umami that enriches the sour cream gravy.
  • Off-Heat Sour Cream Swirl: Whisking full-fat sour cream off direct boiling heat ensures a mirror-smooth sauce that never curdles or separates.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil Egg Noodles: Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add wide egg noodles and cook for 6 minutes until tender al dente. Drain and toss with 1/2 tbsp butter to prevent sticking.
  2. Flash-Sear Sirloin Strips: Pat sliced steak dry with paper towels; season with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide heavy skillet over smoking high heat. Add steak strips in an even layer and sear undisturbed for 90 seconds, then toss for 30 seconds more until browned but still rosy in the center. Immediately transfer beef to a plate.
  3. Sauté Mushrooms & Shallots: Melt remaining butter in the same skillet over medium-high heat. Add sliced mushrooms and shallots; sauté for 3 minutes until mushrooms are deeply caramelized and tender. Stir in minced garlic for 30 seconds.
  4. Simmer Savory Gravy Base: Pour in beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, and Dijon mustard, scraping up all the savory browned bits from the pan bottom. Simmer vigorously for 2 minutes until reduced slightly.
  5. Swirl Sour Cream & Toss Noodles: Reduce skillet heat to low. Stir in room-temperature sour cream until fully emulsified into a velvety creamy gravy. Return seared steak strips and accumulated juices to the pan to warm through for 60 seconds. Add the drained egg noodles directly into the skillet (or serve beef over noodles). Garnish with fresh dill and cracked black pepper. Serve hot!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Never boil sour cream over high heat! High temperatures cause the delicate milk proteins in sour cream to break and curdle into grainy specks. Always lower your heat to gentle warmth (or pull the pan off the burner) before whisking in the sour cream for an ultra-velvety restaurant finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this with Greek yogurt?
Yes, whole-milk plain Greek yogurt works well, but be extra careful with heat as Greek yogurt is even more heat-sensitive than sour cream.
What if my sauce is too thin?
Whisk 1 teaspoon of cornstarch with 1 tablespoon of cold water, stir into the simmering broth before adding sour cream, and bubble for 30 seconds until thick.
Can I use canned mushrooms?
Fresh cremini or white button mushrooms are much better—canned mushrooms are waterlogged and will not caramelize into rich umami.
