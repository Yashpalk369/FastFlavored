20-Minute Creamy Thai Coconut Lime Ginger Chicken Skillet

Golden pan-seared chicken cutlets simmering in a fragrant, velvety coconut milk, fresh lime zest, ginger, and garlic sauce in 20 minutes.

Bottom Line Up Front (Quick Answer):

To make 20-minute creamy coconut lime chicken, season 4 chicken cutlets with salt and pepper, then sear in 1 tbsp coconut oil over medium-high heat for 4 minutes per side until golden; set aside. In the same skillet, sauté 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger, and 1 sliced red chili for 1 minute. Pour in 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk, 1 tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce), 1 tsp honey, and the zest and juice of 1 fresh lime. Simmer for 4 minutes until slightly thickened. Return chicken to skillet to coat, and garnish with fresh cilantro leaves.

Why This Recipe Conquers Weeknights

  • Full-Fat Culinary Coconut Milk: Canned full-fat coconut milk creates a luscious dairy-free emulsion that coats chicken cutlets with silky luxury.
  • Fresh Lime Zest Oil Release: Zesting the lime directly into sizzling ginger extracts aromatic citrus oils that give this sauce its intoxicating restaurant fragrance.
  • Umami Balance via Fish Sauce: A single tablespoon of fish sauce supplies deep, savory umami that balances coconut sweetness without tasting fishy.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sear Golden Chicken Cutlets: Pat chicken cutlets dry; season both sides with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp coconut oil in a wide heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken cutlets and sear undisturbed for 4 minutes until golden brown. Flip and sear 3 minutes more until just cooked through. Remove to a warm plate.
  2. Sauté Aromatics in Fond: Lower heat to medium. Add minced garlic, grated ginger, sliced red chili, ground coriander, and turmeric into the remaining pan oil. Sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant and aromatic.
  3. Build Velvet Coconut Lime Sauce: Pour in the full-fat coconut milk, fish sauce, honey, and fresh lime zest. Whisk well to dissolve any browned bits from the pan. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 4 to 5 minutes until the sauce reduces into a silky, luscious consistency.
  4. Finish with Lime Juice & Simmer: Squeeze fresh lime juice into the simmering sauce and stir. Return the seared chicken cutlets and all resting juices back into the skillet. Spoon creamy coconut sauce over the chicken for 1 to 2 minutes until heated through.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Scatter fresh chopped cilantro and extra lime wheels over the skillet. Serve piping hot over fluffy steamed jasmine rice or coconut rice!

Elena’s Chef Pro-Tip

Chef Secret: Always zest the lime before juicing it, and add the zest into the sizzling aromatics while saving the fresh lime juice for the final 60 seconds of simmering. Boiling lime juice for too long dulls its bright floral aroma, whereas adding it right at the end keeps the sauce vibrantly fresh.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I substitute chicken thighs?
Absolutely! Boneless skinless chicken thighs are wonderful in this recipe and remain succulent. Brown them for 5 minutes per side.
What if I don't eat fish sauce?
You can easily substitute 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or tamari plus a tiny pinch of salt to maintain that savory umami balance.
Is this recipe gluten-free and dairy-free?
Yes! This recipe is 100% naturally gluten-free and dairy-free, relying entirely on wholesome full-fat coconut milk for its luxurious richness.
